AIV numerical experiments

Numerical orbit-space profiles. These coordinates are the radii of higher-dimensional spherical orbits. They are not plane curves evolving by ordinary curve shortening. The eight-dimensional type II time model is explicitly formal.

Numerical method and limitations

Profiles are precomputed with DOP853. Shrinkers use matching of axis-regular and inward asymptotic integrations; expanders use scalar shooting to match cone slopes. Larger cutoffs, tighter tolerances and an independent differential-defect calculation are recorded in the numerical audit. These comparisons are not certified error bounds. Time scaling is exact for the underlying solitons; the stored spatial coordinates remain numerical. The complete fattened level set and the nonlinear eight-dimensional flow have not been computed.
