# Requested literature additions

This expansion adds 62 canonical reading pages and extends two existing pages. McGrath’s compact product-of-spheres shrinker is excluded, including the overlapping g=2 case of the Riedler family and its use as a prescribed translator end. The four primary categories and permanent labels of retained content are unchanged.

## Source review

The source manifest records the main theorem, equation, section or figure inspected for each construction. This is a comparison of selected source statements and calculations, not a claim that every proof in the literature has been independently verified. Reviewed arXiv PDFs are fingerprinted in the source inventory; no PDFs are redistributed. The Liao multiphase proposal is explicitly recorded as a reported, unverified construction. Recent preprints remain attributed preprint results.

## Geometry and time

Every addition has three times or stages at a common spatial scale, with rotation, zoom and a single-time display. Exact homothety or isometric motion of a schematic does not make its shape an exact soliton. Non-self-similar constructions lacking computable finite-time slices use explicitly labeled stages. Higher-dimensional pictures are profiles, selected slices or projections. Curved ambient metrics are stated separately; average-curvature conventions are converted when physical time is assigned.

Numerical profiles use the equations stated on the reading pages. Symmetric networks are shot to the reflection condition and retain 120-degree junction angles. Anciaux profiles are shot to a rational phase increment. The graphical expander is a finite-domain explicit MCF computation, compared on 81×81 and 161×161 grids. The recorded differences are sampled numerical diagnostics, not error bounds or existence proofs.

Regenerate content with `.venv/bin/python scripts/integrate_literature_additions.py`. Regenerate figures with `python3 scripts/generate_literature_figures.py` in an environment with NumPy and SciPy. The data-driven viewer is `site/assets/literature-flow-viewer.mjs`.
