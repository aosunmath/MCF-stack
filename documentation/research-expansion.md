# Expansion from Ao Sun's research papers

This expansion brought the catalogue to 42 example families; later additions are recorded in [the prescribed-tangent-flow reference expansion](prescribed-tangent-shrinkers.md). This addition follows [Ao Sun's research page](https://sites.google.com/view/aosun/research) to primary papers and their construction references. It adds 11 entries, while preserving the five-chapter structure and the tags of the original 31 examples.

| Category | New constructions |
| --- | --- |
| Shrinkers | Kapouleas–Kleene–Møller one-ended examples; Drugan–Nguyen variational tori; Buzano–Nguyen–Schulz arbitrary-genus examples; Ketover doubled planes; Kapouleas–McGrath spherical doublings |
| Translators | Sun–Wang translators with Angenent-torus ends, including the nonunique minimizing completions; translators with high-genus spherical-doubling ends |
| Other special flows | Ancient torus curves and eternal helices; Chen–Sun immortal annuli with a double-plane limit; compact immortal product-manifold flows with a double-sphere limit |

The Stryker–Sun torus-curve entry retains the earlier Altschuler–Altschuler–Angenent–Wu construction credit. The compact curve is one-dimensional despite lying on a product torus. The exact helix and torus-curve formulas have time controls; their scalar time relations are numerically inverted. The torus curve is displayed through a labeled four-to-three-dimensional projection. Noncompact helices are truncated to a stated parameter interval. Both viewers retain a fixed physical viewing scale through time.

The other nine new figures are original diagrams of topology, asymptotics, gluing inputs, or orbit-space profiles. They are labeled schematics, not numerical solutions of the existence constructions. The sufficiently-large-genus conditions on the BNS and Ketover end theorems are retained. Different construction families are not asserted to give noncongruent surfaces without a theorem establishing that distinction.

The [source audit](research-expansion-sources.json) records exact versions, PDF hashes, checked locators, and mathematical qualifications. Only selected relevant passages were checked. Source PDFs, extraction, and rendered paper pages remain private and are not redistributed. The original supplied-paper inventory and its first-batch audit remain separate.

The [property browser](https://aosunmath.github.io/MCF-stack/browse/) covers every example, including the original collection. Its [metadata guide](catalogue-properties.md) explains case-specific ambient dimensions, surface genus, ends, entropy, and Gaussian area. The numerical Angenent entropy is distinguished from the proved bound for the variational torus. Fixed-time entropy of the helix is derived directly in the entry, separately from the cited unbounded supremum over time. The Chen–Sun product-manifold construction is not labeled a Euclidean mean curvature flow.

All new drawings have reproducible generators listed in [the illustration guide](catalogue-expansion.md). `site/assets/solitons/ancient-curve-audit.json` records scalar-inversion residuals; these are numerical sampling diagnostics rather than existence proofs. The interactive formulas are tested against their vector curve-shortening equations.

The Ketover doubled-plane figure has since been replaced by an interactive
two-sheet topology model with individual necks and a cutaway; see
[the source review and drawing specification](ketover-picture.md).
