# Joyce Tsai Lee Zhu Tsui Wang and Su source review

The November 2026 review adds fifteen illustrated families to MCF-stack and a classification cross-reference to the existing graphical grim-reaper chains. It checks the construction statements and the passages needed for the recorded formulas, topology, dimensions and geometric properties. It is a selected literature review, not an exhaustive classification or a formal proof audit. Public source PDFs remain outside the repository and exports.

The requested names are Dominic Joyce, Chung-Jun Tsai, Tang-Kai Lee, Jonathan J. Zhu, Mao-Pei Tsui, Mu-Tao Wang and Wei-Bo Su. Yng-Ing Lee, Ping-Hung Lee and Jingze Zhu are distinct coauthors and are not substitutions for the requested authors. The [machine-readable source audit](joyce-tsai-lee-zhu-sources.json) records publication-list URLs, exact editions and checked passages; the manuscript source inventory also records PDF hashes and page counts.

## Primary sources and mathematical qualifications

- [Joyce–Lee–Tsui, 0801.3721v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/0801.3721v2): Theorems C–D give the integral neck formulas. The compact entry uses the nonconstant periodic case of Theorem E(a)(ii), with rational phase-closing conditions. Corollary I gives the small-angle translators. A constant-radius specialization of Theorem G/Corollary H gives the explicitly parametrized rotating family. The periodic compact theorem supplies immersions; it does not label every closing datum embedded.
- [Lee–Wang, 0707.0239v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/0707.0239v1) and [Braxton–Lee–Zhu, 2609.19648v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19648v1): the Möbius surface is counted on its embedded quotient, not the double-cover cylinder. The recent stability theorem is stated with its source's variational meaning; no generic-singularity theorem is inferred. The exact entropy involves modified Bessel functions and is not replaced by an unverified rounded bound.
- [Lee–Wang, 0802.0359v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/0802.0359v1): the new mixed-sign entries restrict to unit weights, intrinsic dimension at least three, and positive weight sum. The exact quotient is a mapping torus under the antipodal map. The product shorthand and orientation claim in Proposition 2.2 need qualification: a disconnected fiber exchanged by the monodromy can give an orientable total manifold even when the ambient complex dimension is odd. In particular, the three-dimensional expander has topology R² × S¹. These statements were checked from the quotient directly. Other integer weights and varifold multiplicities are outside these entries.
- [Imagi–Joyce–Oliveira dos Santos, 1404.0271v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.0271v2): uniqueness statements retain dimension at least three, exactness, embeddedness, closedness as a subset, and the required asymptotic rates. The special Lagrangian and expander theorems have different phase-angle and rate hypotheses.
- [Tsai–Tsui–Wang, 2309.09432v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.09432v2): Proposition 6.5 supplies expanders from homogeneous two-convex initial potentials, with the uniform gradient-graph volume-form bound. The displayed nonquadratic potential is an independently checked concrete seed satisfying the hypotheses. The mesh is the initial gradient cone, not the positive-time PDE solution. Two-convexity here is the paper's Hessian-pair condition, not hypersurface mean convexity.
- [Aryan–Lee, 2608.04855v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04855v1): Theorem 5.1 gives a nonround antipodally invariant metric arbitrarily close to the round sphere and one immortal embedded curve with an entire circle of geodesic limits. This is not a counterexample for the exactly round metric.
- [Su–Tsai–Wood, 2401.02228v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.02228v3): the Main Theorem and Section 9 concern two special Lagrangian tori meeting once at a type-one transverse intersection, in a flat complex torus of complex dimension at least three. The neck collapses only at infinite time. The regular connected sum is embedded; its singular limiting union is not assigned that status.
- [Ping-Hung Lee–Tsai, 2606.28767v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28767v1): Theorem 6.2 retains the collinear centers, slope, concavity and weighted second-derivative hypotheses. It yields vanishing mean curvature with diverging second fundamental form in a Gibbons–Hawking ambient space. The quotient graph diagram is not planar curve shortening.
- [Su, 2011.07882v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.07882v2): the Main Theorem and final perturbation argument give the embedded two-cylinder family in complex dimension at least three. Corollary 1.1 permits more ends and loops for suitable finite arrangements. The T-finite condition excludes an unproved claim about gluing arbitrary planes and cylinders. The gluing parameter is distinct from translation time.
- [Su–Wood, 2507.01345v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.01345v1): equation (31), Theorem 5.6 and Proposition 5.9 give the new stationary SO(m−1) family. The formulas use the real Liouville primitive B(s), avoiding ambiguity from conjugation in text extraction. The line/expander translator recovers the symmetric JLT case. Line/shrinker and expander/shrinker translators are additional constructions in this source, but are outside this selected addition; other symmetry groups can introduce a one-dimensional singular set.
- [Stolarski–Su, 2607.03152v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.03152v1): Theorem 1.1 and Remark 1 give the reviewed SO(n) subfamily. The allowed integer rates are K ≥ 2 for n ≥ 4, and K = 2, 3, 4 for n = 3. The multiplicity-one tangent pair of planes and the finer Lawlor-neck blow-up are different limits. The exact profile in the entry belongs to the limit, not the evolving solution. The source's compact immersed variant is not incorporated.
- [Choi–Seo–Su–Zhao, 2603.09112v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09112v1): Theorem 1.1 and Corollary 1.5 are cross-linked to the existing Angenent–You graphical chains. The hypothesis is finite supremum of entropy over all flow times. For the selected three-reaper family that supremum is four; the per-slice property records only the resulting upper bound.

## Figures and checks

`scripts/generate_lagrangian_catalogue.py` reproduces the new illustrations with the Python standard library. Six finite meshes can be rotated as projections from four dimensions. Other figures are explicitly labeled exact real slices, auxiliary profiles, construction diagrams or schematics. Simpson quadrature refines the integral examples; its reported difference is a diagnostic, not a certified error bound.

`tests/test_lagrangian_geometry.py` independently checks selected soliton equations, the Möbius quotient seam and boundary, and the homogeneous initial seed. Registry tests distinguish nonorientable surfaces from higher-dimensional manifolds. Publication uses the full Python and JavaScript suites, the link-checked build, and desktop/mobile reader checks required by the integration contract. Review entries remain `cited-example`; no formal-verification badges are added.
