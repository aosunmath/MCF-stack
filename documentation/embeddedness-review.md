# Embeddedness classification review

Reviewed on November 6, 2026 against the statements and references in the four active catalogue manuscripts. All 52 families and 98 existing geometric cases now have an explicit embeddedness status and scope. Existing case order and case anchors are preserved.

The 47 families marked `embedded` describe embeddings in their full stated ambient spaces, including the Buzano–Nguyen–Schulz surfaces of every positive genus. The classification follows the referenced existence statements or the injectivity of the explicit parametrizations. For the ancient torus curves, the recorded restriction is `k₁ = 1`; for the helices, the last coordinate is the real parameter. Crossings in projections of higher-codimension examples are not self-intersections of the original embeddings. Gluing configurations and multiplicity-two limits are not substituted for the smooth examples.

| Family | Status | Scope and evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Noncircular Abresch–Langer curves | Not embedded | The primitive noncircular curves in the catalogue have self-intersections; the circle is a separate entry. See the Abresch–Langer entry and its classification references. |
| Drugan spheres | Not embedded | The cited theorem constructs self-intersecting rotational sphere immersions in every recorded dimension. See Drugan, Theorem 1.1, and Drugan–Lee–Nguyen, Theorem 3, as cited in the entry. |
| Whitney-sphere flow | Not embedded | The initial immersion has a transverse double point. Its regular flow remains Lagrangian with sphere domain; the obstruction to an embedded Lagrangian sphere applies throughout the smooth existence interval. [Viana, pp. 1–3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1802.02108v2) records the initial double point, the obstruction, and preservation of the Lagrangian and equivariant conditions. |
| Products of closed shrinking curves | Depends on the construction | The product map is embedded exactly when every factor is an embedded, singly covered circle. A self-intersecting factor or multiple cover makes the product map nonembedded. The criterion is recorded in the existing product entry. |
| Spherical minimal shrinkers | Depends on the construction | Composing a minimal immersion into the sphere with the Euclidean inclusion preserves whether the map is an embedding. The displayed products of round spheres are embedded; the general input is an immersion. See the spherical-minimal entry and its Smoczyk reference. |
| Expanding tree networks | Embedded, in the network sense | Branches are embedded and intersect only at their prescribed endpoints. Regular junctions have three branches at 120 degrees; the network is not a smooth curve at those points. [Ilmanen–Neves–Schulze, Introduction and Section 2](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10064621/1/Schulze_Networkflow.pdf) states the convention. |

No current case is assigned `not-recorded`; the status remains available for future additions lacking a recorded conclusion. Construction-dependent cases are kept separate from both definite filters rather than assigning every member a single yes/no answer. The reader retains the full references in each example page.
