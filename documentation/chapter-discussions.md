## Mathematical distinctions retained

Tangent flows versus general curvature-scale limits; entropy versus centered Gaussian area; fixed-center Gaussian stability versus F-stability and entropy stability; weighted translator stability versus dynamical stability; smooth output in low dimensions versus possible high-dimensional singular sets; prescribed parabolic ends versus unscaled cylindrical ends; expander uniqueness versus isotopy; finite-time embeddedness versus limiting multiplicity; Euclidean flow versus flow in a curved ambient metric. Relative expander entropy may be positive infinite for a general obstacle competitor.
