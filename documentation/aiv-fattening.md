## Conventions and checked coordinate corrections

Write `x=|X|`, `y=|Y|` in `R^p × R^q`. For `y=u(x,t)`, the reduced MCF is

```math
u_t=\frac{u_{xx}}{1+u_x^2}+\frac{p-1}{x}u_x-\frac{q-1}{u}.
```

All solitons use `Hvec = −Xperp/2` (shrinker) or `Hvec = Xperp/2` (expander), with flows `sqrt(−t) Sigma` for `t<0` and `sqrt(t) Sigma` for `t>0`.

The computations use equations (2), (6), (8), derived consistently from this graph equation. Several draft displays have inconsistent factors or coordinates. We do not copy those into code:

- The minimal cone is `y/x=sqrt((q−1)/(p−1))`, as in equation (11). The sentence before Theorem 1 defines the reciprocal ratio but prints this value for it.
- The positive-time expanders in (38)–(39) use `sqrt(t)`, not the printed `sqrt(−t)`.
- In the inverse graph `x=v(y,t)` with `(p,q)=(2,6)`, the equation is `v_t=v_yy/(1+v_y²)+5v_y/y−1/v`. This agrees with (42) and (75). It gives the stationary inner ODE `G''/(1+G'²)+5G'/z−1/G=0`, asymptotic to `z/sqrt(5)`. The coefficients in (54)–(55) describe the opposite graph and cannot be used with the inverse-coordinate asymptote (57).
- The base cone in (50) must be `eta/sqrt(5)`, consistently with (43), (51), and the inverse graph PDE. The intermediate eigenfunction and eigenvalue are checked directly against (45), independent of the inconsistent sign in the preceding separation ansatz.

These are local consistency calculations, not a claim to have repaired all arguments of the unfinished draft.

## Reproduction

The generator needs Python 3, NumPy, SciPy and Matplotlib. They are numerical-generation dependencies; site builds and asset tests do not need them.

```sh
python3 scripts/generate_aiv_fattening.py
python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_aiv_fattening.py'
node --test tests/aiv-viewer.test.mjs
```

The implementation is [generate_aiv_fattening.py](../scripts/generate_aiv_fattening.py). The viewer is [aiv-viewer.mjs](../site/assets/aiv-viewer.mjs). The [JSON audit](../site/assets/solitons/aiv-audit.json) contains solver tolerances, cutoff comparisons, matching residuals and independently differenced ODE defects. The full-page viewers embed their data and script so they work in the reader's sandboxed iframe without cross-origin fetches.

For `sign = −1,0,+1` (shrinking, stationary, expanding), solve

```math
U''=(1+U'^2)\left(\frac{q-1}{U}-\frac{p-1}{x}U'
+\frac{\mathrm{sign}}2(U-xU')\right).
```

At the axis, `U(0)=a`, `U'(0)=0` and `U''(0)=((q−1)/a+sign*a/2)/p`. Integration starts at `epsilon=min(10^−4 a,10^−5)` with the quadratic Taylor value and its derivative; refined profiles halve the relative axis cutoff. DOP853 uses relative tolerance `2e−11`, refined to `2e−13`, and absolute tolerance one hundredth as large.

Direct outward shrinker shooting excites a growing mode. Instead, integrate from the axis to `x=1.5` and inward from `L=32`, refined to `L=40`. Solve for the axis radius and cone slope so that both height and derivative agree. The far boundary uses `U=Ax+B/x+C/x³`, with coefficients obtained by substituting the shrinker ODE. Matching a finite boundary is not a proof of a complete shrinker. The existence statement is attributed to the source.

For expanders, estimate the cone slope from `(U(L)/L+U'(L))/2+C/L⁴`, cancelling the leading two asymptotic corrections. Brent shooting matches this estimate to the shrinker's slope. Each radius selection in the viewer has its own integrated data; changing the radius does not incorrectly dilate a normalized expander.

## Observed numerical results

For `(p,q)=(2,2)`, the two shrinker approximations have:

| Resolved cone-crossing index | Axis radius | Cone slope | Slope change, L=32 to 40 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2 | 0.109342076645 | 1.022888697208 | 4.0e−9 |
| 3 | 0.010172969539 | 0.999357615575 | 1.3e−10 |

Three expander candidates match the second slope: two y-caps with radii about `0.07186041748`, `0.66451802376`, and an x-cap with radius about `0.67230355112`. Their fitted slope mismatches are below `4e−14`, but this is a root-solver residual, not the accuracy of the true infinite-domain slope. Radius changes under cutoff/tolerance refinement are below `2e−9`. The largest independently differenced relative ODE defect among these three is below `7e−10`.

For `(p,q)=(2,6)`, the expander with `a=0.1` has estimated slope `2.236065103386`, about `2.8741e−6` below `sqrt(5)`. The slope changes by less than `8e−13` under the recorded refinement. The stationary y-cap crosses the Simoes cone near the displayed region, whereas its opposite cap provides the stationary inverse-coordinate model. These are floating-point observations, not a replacement for Appendix A's barriers or Lemma 23's hypotheses.

The other selections compute 28 expander profiles and seven stationary cap profiles. The maximum profile change under the recorded refinements is less than `1e−7` for expanders and `3e−10` for stationary caps; the audit retains the per-case values. The finite output window is not a boundary of a complete solution. Gaussian area and entropy are not estimated from these truncated meshes.

The eight-dimensional time control applies the predicted inner scale `|t|^(2/3)` for the formal `k=2` model to a stationary inverse cap. This rescaled family alone does **not** solve MCF. No complete negative-time nonlinear construction, matched outer solution, positive-time type II solution, or maximal fattened set is numerically computed. The viewer labels that limitation continuously.
