## Evidence and limits

The [machine-readable audit](author-paper-search.json) records exact downloaded editions, SHA-256 hashes, and checked theorem or page locators. New cited preprints are also registered in the manuscript source inventory. The original PDFs, extracted text, and source figures remain outside the repository and are not redistributed. The [addition register](author-expansion-entries.json) maps the twelve new permanent labels to their reading routes.

The November 2026 knotted-expander paper is identified as a preprint. The stacked-pancake record uses the current July 2026 v3 author list, Mramor and Yudowitz, and retains n ≥ 3. The current semigraphical classification is Martín–Sáez–Tsiamis–White, arXiv:2411.16889v2; it supersedes 2310.06980. The earlier pitchfork uniqueness disclaimer has been replaced, retaining the theorem hypotheses. Annuloids have two topological ends and entropy 4; their four vertical asymptotic planes are not counted as four ends.

The ancient minimal-surface entry includes the early normal graphs over the Costa surface only as its property case; its broader cited existence theorem is not used to assign Costa topology to every input minimal hypersurface. The reapernoid has separate surface and higher-dimensional cases because its pointed forward limits differ. Smooth mountain-pass expanders are restricted to 2 ≤ n ≤ 6. The high-genus expander entry keeps the special three-component cone and prime-power genus restrictions. Topological deductions from parametrizations or graph constructions are identified as such.

### Revised stacked-pancake illustration

The stacked-pancake entry now has an original shaded, rotatable three-dimensional
linear section and an annotated meridian. The source review used
[Mramor–Yudowitz, arXiv:2509.26515v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.26515v3),
Theorem 1.1, Figure 1, the rotational equation (2.6), and the construction in
Section 3.1 (including Figure 2). The construction selects a neck parameter in
long-lived approximating flows and takes an ancient limit; it does not supply an
explicit time slice. The introduction specifically leaves the precise backward
pancake/catenoid decomposition unresolved.

Run `python3 scripts/generate_stacked_pancakes.py` to reproduce both SVGs and the
viewer mesh; the author-survey generator delegates to this script as well. The
Bezier profile is a qualitative drawing with two radial maxima, one positive
neck minimum, reflection symmetry, and two capped poles. Its axial coordinate is
strictly monotone. Mesh checks verify a closed sphere section before any display
cutaway is applied. Proportions, neck color, and angular cutaway are illustrative;
there is no time slider, PDE solution, or asserted catenoid neck. Full orbits are
S^(n−1), collapsing at the poles; only an S^1 section is rendered. Thus the 3D
section is not an n=2 example: equation (2.6) retains the full hypersurface dimension
n ≥ 3. The second drawing identifies the fixed containing slab separately from
the moving poles. Both drawings remain available without JavaScript and in print.
