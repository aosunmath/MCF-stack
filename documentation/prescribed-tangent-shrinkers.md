# Shrinkers from the prescribed-tangent-flow references

This expansion follows the introduction and bibliography of [Chen–Lee–Sun–Zhao, *Closed Mean Curvature Flows with Prescribed Tangent Flows*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23879v1). It adds ten shrinker construction entries, bringing the collection to **52 families, including 25 shrinker entries**, within the existing five chapters. All earlier permanent tags are retained.

| Added entry | Main recorded information |
| --- | --- |
| Nguyen shrinking saddles | Genus m−1 for sufficiently large m; one conical end; source normalization converted by √2 |
| Angenent torus cylinders | Exact product T²×ℝᵏ; dimension k+2; two ends for k=1 and one for k≥2; exact Gaussian factorization |
| Ilmanen–White | Every genus h≥2, two conical ends, and realization by a critical wheel |
| Shao–Zou | Any prescribed k≥2 ends; genus (k−1)(m−1) for sufficiently large m |
| Hoffman–Martín–White ray-avoiding shrinkers | Platonic and regular-polygon symmetries; genus determined by the number of excluded rays |
| Hoffman–Martín–White sphere–plane family | Every positive genus; entropy below a chosen δ; exactly one conical end for large genus |
| Hoffman–Martín–White three-ended family | Large even genus, three conical ends, multiplicity-three plane limit |
| Ketover Platonic shrinkers | Five genera 3, 5, 7, 11, 19 and the strict bound 4/e<λ<8/e |
| Sun–Wang–Zhou | Gaussian-width shrinkers in dimensions 2–6, with unbounded entropy and index bounds; a separate product extension in higher dimensions |
| Chu–Sun | Genus one and entropy strictly below the Drugan–Nguyen torus, without a prescribed shape or end count |

Each entry identifies its construction method, ambient and intrinsic dimensions, normalization, source-supported topology, symmetries, ends, entropy information, and unresolved properties. The list counts construction entries, not proven noncongruent families: agreement of topology or asymptotic limits does not identify two constructions, and different methods do not prove they differ.

Compactness and ends remain unspecified for the Platonic min-max limits, the general Gaussian-width examples, and the Chu–Sun genus-one example. A compact sweepout or numerical picture does not prove that the limiting shrinker is compact. HMW's constructions in Sections 7–8 start with intrinsic flows in S²×ℝ; their tangent shrinkers are Euclidean. The paper's closed-Euclidean-flow realization theorem is invoked separately for the torus cylinders.

The [source audit](prescribed-tangent-shrinker-sources.json) records the exact eight PDF editions, hashes, and checked theorem/page locators. Seven are new bibliography records; the previously checked Chu–Sun edition is reused. The audit covers selected relevant passages, not complete independent proof verification. Downloaded PDFs, extracted text, and rendered paper pages are kept out of the published repository.

Nine new illustrations are original schematics of topology, construction inputs, symmetry, or a proved growth relation. They are generated by `scripts/generate_referenced_shrinker_schematics.py` and are explicitly not computed shrinkers. The torus-cylinder viewer, generated by `scripts/generate_torus_cylinder_slices.py`, shows three two-dimensional slices of a connected three-dimensional product in ℝ⁴. It inherits the numerical Angenent mesh and offers rotation, zoom, and fourth-coordinate projection. It does not draw the whole product or three different components. There are now 21 interactive illustrations in the collection.

All ten entries have case-specific records in `catalogue-properties.json`. In particular, the Shao–Zou entry appears under “At least four ends,” unknown end counts are not converted to zero, and genus of a torus factor is not assigned as surface genus to its higher-dimensional product. Numerical Angenent entropy remains excluded from the proved-bound filter.
