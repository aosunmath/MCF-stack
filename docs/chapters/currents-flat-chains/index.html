Chapter 5

Currents and flat chains9250EFD1531A

Mass, compactness, flat chains, pushforwards, homotopies, slicing, Plateau existence, calibrated currents, and singular complex-curve minimizers. Scalar-to-vector Riesz representation, comass duality, and a direct sequential compactness proof. Tangential Lipschitz calculus, geometric and normal slicing, flat continuity and exceptional levels. Analytic and geometric Lipschitz images, signed multiplicities, and integer rectifiability under convergence in mass.
