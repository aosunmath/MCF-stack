Corollary 17.4.3 (Equality in Weiss monotonicity)A3D7C291127B

Corollary 17.4.3 (Equality in Weiss monotonicity).A3D7C291127B For $u$ as in Theorem 17.4.2, the Weiss energy is constant on an interval $(a,b)\subset (0,R)$ if and only if

\[ x\cdot Du(x)=2u(x)\quad (a<|x|<b). \]

Equivalently, along every ray the quotient $u(r\omega )/r^2$ is constant while $a<r<b$. Constancy on $(0,b)$ means that $u$ is homogeneous of degree two in $B_ b$.

Proof

If the energy is constant, the nonnegative derivative formula makes $x\cdot Du-2u$ vanish on almost every sphere in the annulus. Coarea and continuity make it vanish everywhere there. Along a ray, $\frac d{dr}(r^{-2}u(r\omega ))=r^{-3}(r\omega \cdot Du-2u)$, which proves the homogeneity assertion. Conversely that identity makes the derivative of $W$ zero almost everywhere; absolute continuity makes $W$ constant. This gives the equality case of Theorem 17.4.2. $\square$
