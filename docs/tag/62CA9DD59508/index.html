Corollary 9.11.6 (Flat continuity of almost-everywhere geometric slices)62CA9DD59508

Corollary 9.11.6 (Flat continuity of almost-everywhere geometric slices).62CA9DD59508 If $T$ is compactly supported and integral, choose the full-measure set of good levels from Proposition 9.10.5 and Proposition 9.10.6. At a good level $t$ with $\mu (\{ f=t\} )=0$, every sequence of good levels $t_ j\to t$ satisfies

\[ \mathcal F_{\mathbb R}(S_{t_ j}-S_ t)\longrightarrow 0. \]

This conclusion concerns flat convergence, and does not assert convergence of slice masses. It combines the geometric slicing theorem with the exceptional-level distinction in [Sim18], Lemma 4.5 and equations (4.6)–(4.7).

Proof

At each good level $S_ t=B_ t^-$. Theorem 9.11.4 makes the latter map continuous at the stated non-atomic level. Substituting the equal currents proves the claim, regardless of which side the sequence approaches from. $\square$
