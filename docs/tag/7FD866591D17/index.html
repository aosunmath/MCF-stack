Remark 16.1.4 (Multiplicity, curvature, and index in dimension three)7FD866591D17

Remark 16.1.4 (Multiplicity, curvature, and index in dimension three).7FD866591D17 The supplied Chodosh–Mantoulidis paper [CM] is arXiv:1803.02716v2, with a manuscript date of May 16, 2018. Its Sections 3–5 develop curvature estimates, separation of transition sheets, and the relationship between multiplicity and Jacobi fields for solutions with controlled energy and Morse index. Theorem 1.7 explains why a smooth limiting component with multiplicity, or a one-sided component, forces a positive Jacobi field (on the two-sided cover when needed). For bumpy metrics on closed three-manifolds this obstruction gives two-sided multiplicity-one components. The full convergence and index theorems, including their analytic hypotheses, remain external dependencies here. We do not infer any equality of Allen–Cahn and Almgren–Pitts widths from this source discussion.
