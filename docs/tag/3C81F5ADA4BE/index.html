Corollary 10.10.8 (Flat singular points form the top stratum difference)3C81F5ADA4BE

Corollary 10.10.8 (Flat singular points form the top stratum difference).3C81F5ADA4BE The flat singular points are exactly $\mathcal S^ m(V)\setminus \mathcal S^{m-1}(V)$. At each such point the density is an integer $q\geq 2$.

Proof

Failure of membership in $\mathcal S^{m-1}$ means some tangent cone has spine dimension $m$, since Proposition 10.9.4 bounds it above by $m$. Corollary 10.9.8 identifies that cone as $q|P|$. Conversely a multiple plane has spine dimension $m$. The tangent-cone mass formula, Theorem 10.3.6, identifies the density at the original point as $q$. If $q=1$, Corollary 10.4.4 makes the point regular, a contradiction. $\square$
