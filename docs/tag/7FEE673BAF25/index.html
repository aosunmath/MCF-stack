Corollary 8.5.9 (A singular perimeter minimizer in ambient dimension eight)7FEE673BAF25

Corollary 8.5.9 (A singular perimeter minimizer in ambient dimension eight).7FEE673BAF25 The boundary $C_4\subset \mathbb R^8$ is a seven-dimensional locally perimeter-minimizing cone, smooth away from zero and singular at zero. Thus perimeter minimization does not imply everywhere smooth boundary in this dimension.

Proof

Theorem 8.5.8 gives minimization, and Proposition 8.5.6 gives smoothness away from the vertex. If the cone were a smooth hypersurface at zero, its rescalings would converge to its tangent plane. Every rescaling is the same cone, so it would be a plane. It is not: for nonzero $u\in \mathbb R^4$, both $(u,u)$ and $(u,-u)$ belong to it, whereas their sum $(2u,0)$ does not. This proves singularity. The conclusion does not classify other minimizing cones or assert a boundary regularity theorem in lower dimensions. $\square$
