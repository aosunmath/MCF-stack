Proposition 16.10.2 (The Laplacian and the special cancellation for minimal surfaces)022784D6AABA

Proposition 16.10.2 (The Laplacian and the special cancellation for minimal surfaces).022784D6AABA For every smooth function $f$ in the tube,

\[ \Delta f=f_{zz}+H_ zf_ z+\Delta _{g_ z}f. \]

If $\Sigma $ is minimal and $a(y)=|A(y)|^2$, then

\[ D=1-\frac{a z^2}{2},\qquad H_ z=-\frac{az}{1-az^2/2}=-az+O(z^3). \]

For a smooth function $h(y)$ independent of $z$, $\Delta _{g_ z}h=\Delta _\Sigma h+O(|z|)$ uniformly on the fixed patch, with the last constant controlled by its first two derivatives and the local geometry.

Proof

In block diagonal coordinates the divergence formula for the Laplacian separates into $D^{-1}\partial _ z(Df_ z)$ and the tangential Laplace–Beltrami operator. Lemma 16.10.1 gives the coefficient of $f_ z$. For a minimal surface the principal curvatures are $\kappa ,-\kappa $, hence $a=2\kappa ^2$ and $D=1-az^2/2$. Differentiating its logarithm gives the exact rational expression. Its difference from $-az$ is $-a^2z^3/[2(1-az^2/2)]$, uniformly of order $z^3$ on the chosen tube. Finally the coefficients of $\Delta _{g_ z}$ and their first tangential derivatives vary smoothly with $z$. Subtract their values at zero in the coordinate formula; every difference is bounded by $C|z|$. This gives the last estimate without asserting an equality between the metrics at nonzero $z$. $\square$
