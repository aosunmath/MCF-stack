Lemma 6.3.3 (Positivity gives continuity on a fixed support)059D87E3EFB0

Lemma 6.3.3 (Positivity gives continuity on a fixed support).059D87E3EFB0 If $L$ is positive, $K\subset U$ is compact, and $\eta \in C_ c(U)$ is nonnegative and at least one on $K$, then

\[ |L(f)|\leq L(\eta )\| f\| _\infty \quad \text{for every }f\in C_ c(U)\text{ supported in }K. \]

Thus uniform convergence with a common compact support implies convergence of the functional values.

Proof

The pointwise inequalities $-\| f\| _\infty \eta \leq f\leq \| f\| _\infty \eta $ and positivity give the bound. Apply it to the difference of two functions for the continuity assertion. This is [Sim18], Remark 5.13. $\square$
