Proposition 12.4.4 (Detection is invariant under flat homotopy)F4C1074C9761

Proposition 12.4.4 (Detection is invariant under flat homotopy).F4C1074C9761 Suppose $\Phi _0,\Phi _1:X\to \mathcal Z_ n^0$ are flat-homotopic and both independently satisfy the bounded-mass and no-concentration conditions. Then $\Phi _0$ is a $p$-sweepout if and only if $\Phi _1$ is. The homotopy itself need not satisfy a uniform no-concentration estimate for this cohomological conclusion. Compare [LMN], Section 2.6.

Proof

A homotopy into the cycle space with its flat topology induces the same pullback on singular cohomology at its two endpoints. Thus $\Phi _0^*\bar\lambda ^ p=\Phi _1^*\bar\lambda ^ p$. Nonvanishing is equivalent, and the separately assumed analytic conditions make each endpoint admissible. This argument cannot be used to infer no concentration for an endpoint from flat homotopy alone; Example 12.3.5 explains the missing control. $\square$
