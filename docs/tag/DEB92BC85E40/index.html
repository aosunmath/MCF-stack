Proposition 20.22.3 (The second-order energy of a distance layer in three-space)DEB92BC85E40

Proposition 20.22.3 (The second-order energy of a distance layer in three-space).DEB92BC85E40 Let $\Sigma \subset \mathbb R^3$ be a smooth compact embedded two-sided surface without boundary, and choose an injective symmetric tube $F(y,z)=y+z\nu (y)$, $|z|<\rho $, as in Lemma 16.10.1. For $u_\varepsilon (F(y,z))=q(z/\varepsilon )$ define

\[ m_2=\int _{\mathbb R}t^2\psi (t)^2dt\in (0,\infty ). \]

The energy on this tube satisfies

\[ E_\varepsilon (u_\varepsilon ;\operatorname {Tube}_\rho (\Sigma )) =c_ W\operatorname {Area}(\Sigma ) +\varepsilon ^2m_2\int _\Sigma K_\Sigma \, d\mu +O(e^{-2\sqrt2\rho /\varepsilon }). \]

There is no linear term in $\varepsilon $.

Proof

The normal coordinate has unit gradient. Hence the energy density times the volume ratio is $\varepsilon ^{-1}\psi (z/\varepsilon )^2(1+Hz+K_\Sigma z^2)$. Change variables $z=\varepsilon t$. On the symmetric interval the term $t\psi (t)^2$ is odd, so its integral is exactly zero. The remaining terms give the exact finite-interval formula

\[ \operatorname {Area}(\Sigma )\int _{-\rho /\varepsilon }^{\rho /\varepsilon }\psi ^2dt +\varepsilon ^2\left(\int _\Sigma K_\Sigma \, d\mu \right) \int _{-\rho /\varepsilon }^{\rho /\varepsilon }t^2\psi ^2dt. \]

The exponential decay $\psi ^2\leq Ce^{-2\sqrt2|t|}$ makes $m_2$ positive and finite. The omitted zeroth moment is $O(e^{-2\sqrt2\rho /\varepsilon })$. The second-moment tail, multiplied by $\varepsilon ^2$, is bounded by $C(\rho ^2+\varepsilon \rho +\varepsilon ^2)e^{-2\sqrt2\rho /\varepsilon }$ and has the same stated order for fixed $\rho $. This proves the formula. It is an expansion for the specified layer on the tube; extending the function to an ambient domain requires a separate tail estimate such as the one in Theorem 16.4.3. $\square$
