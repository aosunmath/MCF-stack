Proposition 18.15.1 (The conformal translator metric)9EAA75CE20C9

Proposition 18.15.1 (The conformal translator metric).9EAA75CE20C9 For fixed $v\in \mathbb R^{n+1}$ and $n\ge 1$, put $h(x)=\langle x,v\rangle $ and $\bar g_ v=e^{2h/n}\bar g_{\rm Eucl}$. On every immersed $n$-hypersurface, the $\bar g_ v$ area measure is $e^ h dA$. Consequently its translator equation is equivalent to $\bar g_ v$ minimality. If $v\ne 0$, this ambient metric is incomplete.

Proof

The induced metric is multiplied by $e^{2h/n}$ in each of $n$ tangent directions; its volume density is multiplied by $e^ h$. Thus equality of the area functionals and Proposition 20.3.8 give equivalence of stationarity. Along the ray $x(t)=-tv/|v|$, $t\ge 0$, the metric length is

\[ \int _0^\infty e^{-|v|t/n}\, dt=\frac n{|v|}<\infty . \]

Points going to infinity along this ray form a metric Cauchy sequence with no limit in Euclidean space, whose usual local topology agrees with that of the smooth positive metric. Hence the metric is incomplete. The calculation does not assert incompleteness of every induced translator metric. $\square$
