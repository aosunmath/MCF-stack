Definition 17.3.4 (The normalized classical obstacle equation)93AD2E417FD0

Definition 17.3.4 (The normalized classical obstacle equation).93AD2E417FD0 In this and the following section a normalized obstacle solution is a function $u\in C^{1,1}_{\mathrm{loc}}(U)$, $U\subset \mathbb R^ n$ open and $n\geq 2$, satisfying

\[ u\geq 0,\qquad \Delta u=\chi _{\{ u>0\} }\quad \text{almost everywhere in }U. \]

Its contact set is $\Lambda =\{ u=0\} $, its positivity set is $O=\{ u>0\} $, and its interior free boundary is $\Gamma =\partial O\cap U$. This is [Fig18], equation (5.2), after subtracting an obstacle with Laplacian $-1$ in the zero-forcing problem.
