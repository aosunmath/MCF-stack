Theorem 14.2.1 (Nurser's low-width results)EDAAB3DCB932

Theorem 14.2.1 (Nurser's low-width results).EDAAB3DCB932 For the mod-two volume spectrum of the unit round three-sphere,

\[ \omega _1=\omega _2=\omega _3=\omega _4=4\pi ,\qquad \omega _5=\omega _6=\omega _7=2\pi ^2, \]
\[ 2\pi ^2<\omega _9<8\pi ,\qquad \omega _{13}\leq 8\pi . \]

Source: [Nurser], Theorem 8, p. 17. The proof uses explicit detecting families and Lusternik–Schnirelmann arguments, and is not included here. In particular, the final inequality is an upper bound, not a determination of $\omega _{13}$. No value of $\omega _8$ is asserted in this entry.
