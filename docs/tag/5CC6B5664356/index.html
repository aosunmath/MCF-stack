Theorem 13.2.2 (Index equality under a critical-set multiplicity hypothesis)5CC6B5664356

Theorem 13.2.2 (Index equality under a critical-set multiplicity hypothesis).5CC6B5664356 Let $g$ be a smooth bumpy metric on a closed connected $M^{n+1}$ with $2\leq n\leq 6$. Use the $\mathbf F$-continuous sweepout convention of [MNIndex]. Let $\Pi $ be a homotopy class of $p$-sweepouts on a fixed finite $p$-dimensional simplicial complex, with $\mathbf L(\Pi )=\omega _ p(M,g)$, and let $\Phi _ i$ be a minimizing sequence in $\Pi $. Suppose that every embedded minimal cycle in its critical set has multiplicity one. Then that critical set contains an embedded minimal cycle $\Sigma $ satisfying

\[ \operatorname {Area}_ g(\Sigma )=\omega _ p(M,g),\qquad \operatorname {index}(\Sigma )=p. \]

Source: [MNIndex], Main Theorem 1.3, p. 5. The proof remains external. The multiplicity assumption concerns the whole indicated critical set, not merely one selected element; this theorem does not by itself prove multiplicity one.
