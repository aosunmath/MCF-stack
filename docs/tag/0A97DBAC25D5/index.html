Lemma 9.13.5 (A smooth globally Lipschitz map bounds the real flat norm)0A97DBAC25D5

Lemma 9.13.5 (A smooth globally Lipschitz map bounds the real flat norm).0A97DBAC25D5 Let $h:\mathbb R^ Q\to \mathbb R^ P$ be smooth and $L$-Lipschitz, and let $X$ be a compactly supported $m$-current of finite real flat norm. Then

\[ \mathcal F_{\mathbb R}(h_\# X) \leq \max \{ L^ m,L^{m+1}\} \, \mathcal F_{\mathbb R}(X). \]

All maps are proper on the compact supports of the currents used in the infimum.

Proof

For any admissible decomposition $X=R+\partial S$, smooth pushforward and boundary commutation give $h_\# X=h_\# R+\partial h_\# S$. Their masses are at most $L^ m\mathbf M(R)$ and $L^{m+1}\mathbf M(S)$ by Proposition 9.3.8. Their supports are compact, being contained in continuous images of compact supports. They are therefore admissible competitors for the target flat norm. Add the bounds and take the infimum. This lemma is stated for smooth maps, so it does not presuppose a Lipschitz pushforward for arbitrary finite-mass competitors that need not be normal. $\square$
