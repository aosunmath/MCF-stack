Proposition 16.3.5 (The limiting energy measure dominates perimeter)0AB8B0DD0379

Proposition 16.3.5 (The limiting energy measure dominates perimeter).0AB8B0DD0379 Assume the hypotheses of Theorem 16.3.3, with $u_ j\to u_ E$ in $L^1(U)$. If a subsequence of the energy measures converges weakly on compactly supported continuous tests in $U$ to $\mu $, then

\[ \mu \geq c_ W|D\chi _ E| \]

as Radon measures on $U$.

Proof

Pass further to the primitive-convergent subsequence in Theorem 16.3.3; this preserves $\mu $ and $u_ E$. For $X\in C_ c^1(U;\mathbb R^ n)$, integration by parts and the pointwise energy inequality give

\[ \left|\int _ U\Phi (u_ j)\operatorname {div}X\right| \leq \int _ U|X|\, d\mu _{\varepsilon _ j}^{u_ j}. \]

Pass to the limit. The resulting bound says that the vector measure $D\Phi (u_ E)$ has total variation at most $\mu $, by the dual characterization of total variation with localized test fields. Proposition 16.3.2 identifies this variation as $c_ W|D\chi _ E|$. Equality is an additional assertion and need not hold. $\square$
