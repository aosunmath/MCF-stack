Proposition 14.3.4 (The conformally invariant energy density modulo curvature)BACD0400946D

Proposition 14.3.4 (The conformally invariant energy density modulo curvature).BACD0400946D For a surface in a space of constant sectional curvature $\kappa $,

\[ \kappa +\tfrac 14|\vec H|^2=K+\tfrac 12|B^0|^2. \]

If an ambient conformal diffeomorphism maps a neighborhood of a closed immersed surface in one space form into another, its Willmore energy with the corresponding ambient curvature term is unchanged.

Proof

In an orthonormal tangent frame the Gauss equation reads

\[ K=\kappa +\langle B_{11},B_{22}\rangle -|B_{12}|^2 =\kappa +\tfrac 12(|\vec H|^2-|B|^2). \]

The orthogonal trace decomposition gives $|B|^2=|B^0|^2+|\vec H|^2/2$, proving the first assertion. Pull back the target ambient metric by the conformal map. Lemma 14.3.2 preserves the trace-free norm density, and Lemma 14.3.3 preserves the integral of Gauss curvature. Integrate the displayed identity on each side. The ambient map must be smooth on a neighborhood of the entire image; a pole lying on the surface is excluded here and is treated separately below. This proof does not require the Gauss–Bonnet theorem. $\square$
