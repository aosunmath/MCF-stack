Proposition 12.6.5 (A continuous time choice with a positive mass drop)8B58860B5F4F

Proposition 12.6.5 (A continuous time choice with a positive mass drop).8B58860B5F4F Fix $0<\tau _0\leq 1$ and put

\[ \tau (V)=\min \{ \tau _0,a(V)/(2B)\} ,\qquad D(V)=\tfrac 12 a(V)\tau (V),\qquad \mathscr H(s,V)=(\varphi _{s\tau (V)}^ V)_\# V. \]

Then $\mathscr H:[0,1]\times K_ C\to K_ C$ is continuous, fixes every stationary varifold, starts at the identity, and has nonincreasing mass in $s$. At its endpoint,

\[ \| \mathscr H(1,V)\| (M)\leq \| V\| (M)-D(V). \]

The continuous function $D$ vanishes exactly on $S_ C$.

Proof

For $0\leq t\leq \tau (V)$, Lemma 12.6.4 gives $A'_ V(t)\leq -a(V)/2$. Integrate from zero to $s\tau (V)$ to obtain monotonicity and the endpoint estimate. For stationary $V$, both the field and the chosen time are zero. For nonstationary $V$, both $a$ and $\tau $ are positive, so $D>0$. Continuity of the fields and times gives $C^1$ continuity of the diffeomorphisms in $(s,V)$. Lemma 12.5.5 gives continuity of $\mathscr H$. Its mass bound shows it stays in $K_ C$. $\square$
