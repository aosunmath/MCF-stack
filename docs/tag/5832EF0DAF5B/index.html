Proposition 10.5.2 (Tangential stationarity forces constant density)5832EF0DAF5B

Proposition 10.5.2 (Tangential stationarity forces constant density).5832EF0DAF5B Let $M$ be a connected $C^2$ Riemannian manifold without boundary and let $\theta \in L^1_{\mathrm{loc}}(M)$. If

\[ \int _ M\theta \operatorname {div}_ M Y\, dA=0 \quad \text{for all compactly supported }C^1\text{ tangent fields }Y, \]

then $\theta $ is constant almost everywhere.

Proof

In coordinates with volume element $\sqrt{g}\, dx$, the divergence is $\operatorname {div}_ M Y=(\sqrt g)^{-1}\partial _ i(\sqrt gY^ i)$. Choose $Y^ i=\phi /\sqrt g$ for one component and zero for the others. The hypothesis becomes $\int \theta \partial _ i\phi \, dx=0$. Lemma 10.5.1 gives a constant on each connected chart. Constants agree on overlaps and hence throughout connected $M$. The volume factor must be included in this coordinate argument; it is absorbed into the chosen test field. Compare [Minter], the second stage of the proof of Lemma 0.7. $\square$
