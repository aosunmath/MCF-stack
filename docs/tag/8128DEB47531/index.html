Proposition 8.3.5 (A local direct method with a bulk term)8128DEB47531

Proposition 8.3.5 (A local direct method with a bulk term).8128DEB47531 Let $U\subset \mathbb R^ n$ be bounded and open, let $K\Subset U$ be compact, let $E_0$ have finite perimeter in $U$, and let $g\in L^1(U)$. Then

\[ \mathcal J_ g(E)=P(E;U)-\int _{E\cap U}g(x)\, dx \]

attains its infimum among measurable sets $E$ with $\chi _ E=\chi _{E_0}$ almost everywhere on $U\setminus K$. The compactly contained set of allowed changes is part of the boundary-data formulation. This extends the constant bulk term in Definition 15.1.1.

Proof

The functional is bounded below by $-\| g\| _{L^1(U)}$, and $E_0$ is an admissible finite-energy competitor. A minimizing sequence therefore has uniformly bounded perimeter. Corollary 8.2.8 gives a subsequence converging locally in $L^1$ and, after a further extraction, almost everywhere. The limit agrees with $E_0$ on $U\setminus K$. Dominated convergence, using $|g\chi _ E|\leq |g|$, shows convergence of the bulk integrals. Perimeter lower semicontinuity then proves attainment. The argument uses no trace-continuity assertion at $\partial U$, because every competitor is fixed outside $K$. $\square$
