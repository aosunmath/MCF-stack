Proposition 6.1.4 (Lower semicontinuity on open sets)E6E21F29D7CA

Proposition 6.1.4 (Lower semicontinuity on open sets).E6E21F29D7CA If $\mu _ j\rightharpoonup \mu $ in $U$, then

\[ \mu (O)\leq \liminf _{j\to \infty }\mu _ j(O) \qquad \text{for every open }O\subset U. \]

This is the open-set inequality accompanying weak convergence in [Sim18], Chapter 1, Section 5.

Proof

Fix $0\leq \varphi \leq 1$ with $\varphi \in C_ c(O)$. Weak convergence gives

\[ \int \varphi \, d\mu =\lim _ j\int \varphi \, d\mu _ j\leq \liminf _ j\mu _ j(O). \]

For every compact $K\subset O$ we can choose such a function with $\varphi =1$ on $K$. Taking the supremum over $K$ and using inner regularity proves the assertion, including the case $\mu (O)=\infty $. $\square$
