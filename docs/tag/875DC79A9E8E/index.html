Proposition 15.10.2 (The rotational CMC system and local uniqueness)875DC79A9E8E

Proposition 15.10.2 (The rotational CMC system and local uniqueness).875DC79A9E8E A rotational arclength immersion has constant trace curvature $H$ precisely when

\[ r'=\cos \psi ,\qquad z'=\sin \psi ,\qquad \psi '=H-\frac{\sin \psi }{r}. \]

For any initial data with $r>0$, this system has a unique smooth local solution. Its first integral is

\[ c=r\sin \psi -\frac H2r^2. \]
Proof

The curvature formula gives the system. Its right side is smooth for $r>0$, so the ordinary local existence and uniqueness theorem applies. Differentiation along a solution yields

\[ c'=\cos \psi \sin \psi +r\cos \psi \left(H-\frac{\sin \psi }{r}\right)-Hr\cos \psi =0. \]

Thus $c$ is constant throughout the interval of existence. The ODE theorem is a background analytic input; this statement gives no continuation through $r=0$. $\square$
