Corollary 10.4.4 (Density one forces a regular point)B253B69E9E98

Corollary 10.4.4 (Density one forces a regular point).B253B69E9E98 If $V$ is stationary and integral and $\Theta ^ n(\| V\| ,a)=1$, then $a$ is regular with multiplicity one. In particular one multiplicity-one planar tangent cone is enough to imply regularity and uniqueness of the tangent cone at $a$.

Proof

At a sufficiently small radius the mass ratio is at most $1+\varepsilon $, while the mean-curvature term is zero. Theorem 10.4.3 gives a $C^{1,\alpha }$ single graph. The stationary graph equation and elliptic bootstrapping make this graph smooth; this stationary consequence is also recorded in [Minter], Corollary 3.2. A multiplicity-one planar tangent cone has density one by Theorem 10.3.6. At a smooth point every blow-up is the ordinary tangent plane with its multiplicity. $\square$
