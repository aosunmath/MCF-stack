Definition 18.6.12 (Cyclic integral Brakke flows)262B8C72DD55

Definition 18.6.12 (Cyclic integral Brakke flows).262B8C72DD55 An integral Brakke flow is cyclic modulo two if at almost every time its associated rectifiable mod-two flat chain satisfies

\[ \partial [V_ t]=0. \]

Here $[V_ t]$ reduces the integer multiplicity modulo two, as in Definition 10.2.2. This property concerns a flat-chain boundary and does not assert that the varifold is orientable or that the flow is unit-regular. See [CCMS20], Section 2.6, and [White09].
