Proposition 17.3.3 (The strong equation and complementarity)171E528F6D01

Proposition 17.3.3 (The strong equation and complementarity).171E528F6D01 Suppose a minimizer $u$ and its obstacle $\psi $ are $C^{1,1}_{\mathrm{loc}}(U)$. Put $v=u-\psi \geq 0$ and $a=-f-\Delta \psi $, with derivatives interpreted almost everywhere. Then

\[ \Delta v=a\chi _{\{ v>0\} },\qquad \lambda =a\chi _{\{ v=0\} }\, dx\geq 0. \]

In particular $a\geq 0$ almost everywhere on contact. This statement assumes the displayed regularity; it does not infer it for arbitrary $L^2$ forcing.

Proof

On $\{ v>0\} $ use the noncontact equation. On $\{ v=0\} $ the preceding lemma gives $\Delta v=0$ almost everywhere. Because $v$ is $C^{1,1}$ locally, its distributional Laplacian has no singular part. Thus the two identities hold distributionally as well. Finally $\lambda =-\Delta u-f=a-\Delta v$ is nonnegative by Proposition 17.2.5. Compare [Fig18], equation (5.1). $\square$
