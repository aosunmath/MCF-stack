Example 3.8.1 (The pitchfork translator)B5E7AB44607B

Example 3.8.1 (The pitchfork translator).B5E7AB44607B The pitchfork is a complete, properly embedded, simply connected, nongraphical translator in $\mathbb R^3$. The supplied notes describe it as a limit of Scherk-type examples, without an explicit formula.

  • It has genus zero. This distinguishes it from Nguyen's periodic infinite-genus tridents despite their similar asymptotic plane arrangements.

  • After reflection to upward translation, it is asymptotic to one plane as $z\to -\infty $ and to three parallel planes as $z\to +\infty $. These are height-direction asymptotics, not a claim that the surface has four topological ends.

  • Its translation yields an eternal noncompact flow. The later construction paper records finite topology and quadratic area growth for its pitchfork subfamily.

See [CatHIMWNotes19], Section 5 and Figure 7, PDF pages 11–12, and [CatHMW20]. The existence proof is external. The supplied notes do not give a complete parameter range or a uniqueness theorem, and the plane diagram is only schematic.
