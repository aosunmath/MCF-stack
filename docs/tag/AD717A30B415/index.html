Example 2.8.1 (Products of closed shrinking curves)AD717A30B415

Example 2.8.1 (Products of closed shrinking curves).AD717A30B415 Let $m\geq 2$ and let each $\gamma _ j:S^1\to \mathbb R^2$ be a normalized closed Abresch–Langer shrinker, including the round circle if desired. Then

\[ X(u_1,\ldots ,u_ m) =(\gamma _1(u_1),\ldots ,\gamma _ m(u_ m))\in \mathbb R^{2m}, \qquad (u_1,\ldots ,u_ m)\in (S^1)^ m \]

satisfies $\vec H=-X^\perp /2$, and $X_ t=\sqrt{-t}\, X$ for $t<0$. The product formula is exact, but any noncircular factor still requires the curvature ODE and closing condition.

  • The domain is the compact $m$-torus. These are complete proper immersions of dimension and codimension $m$, Lagrangian in $\mathbb C^ m$, with flat normal bundle and nowhere vanishing mean-curvature vector. The product metric is intrinsically flat.

  • A product is embedded when every factor is an embedded circle. A noncircular factor has self-intersections, and so does the product immersion. Multiple covers must not be treated as embeddings.

  • Unless all factor images are circles, the image is not contained in a sphere. The all-circle product in $\mathbb R^4$ is the Clifford torus described separately above.

  • These compact products have no ends, finite entropy and a Type I homothetic extinction at the origin. No general stability or uniqueness claim for this family is taken from the cited product construction.
