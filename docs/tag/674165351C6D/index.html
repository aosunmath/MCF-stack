Corollary 16.6.6 (Stable phases on a closed positive-Ricci manifold)674165351C6D

Corollary 16.6.6 (Stable phases on a closed positive-Ricci manifold).674165351C6D On a connected closed Riemannian manifold with positive Ricci curvature, the only smooth stable Allen–Cahn solutions are $u\equiv +1$ and $u\equiv -1$, for every $\varepsilon >0$.

Proof

Use $\eta =1$ in Theorem 16.6.5. The Hessian defect is nonnegative by Lemma 16.6.4; positivity of Ricci therefore forces $\nabla u=0$. Connectedness makes $u$ constant, and the equation permits only $-1,0,1$. The value zero is unstable because $W''(0)=-1$ and the constant test gives $Q_\varepsilon (1)=-\varepsilon ^{-1}\operatorname {Vol}(M)<0$. At the wells $W''(\pm 1)=2$, so stability is immediate. This is the closed-manifold form of [ChAC], Exercise 4.3(b). $\square$
