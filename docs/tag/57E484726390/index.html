Theorem 7.5.2 (Whitney extension for first-order jets)57E484726390

Theorem 7.5.2 (Whitney extension for first-order jets).57E484726390 Every compatible jet in Definition 7.5.1 is the restriction of a function $g\in C^1(\mathbb R^ m)$: $g=h$ and $Dg=a$ on $C$. Conversely, the restriction of a $C^1$ function gives a compatible jet. The existence assertion is a cited theorem, [Sim18], Chapter 2, Theorem 1.6, pp. 46–47, where the proof is referred to [Fed69]. Its extension construction is not proved here. For necessity, the segment formula for $g(y)-g(x)$ and uniform continuity of $Dg$ on the compact convex hull of $K$ give the displayed remainder estimate.
