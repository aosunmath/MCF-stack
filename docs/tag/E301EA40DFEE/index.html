Corollary 16.4.8 (The unconstrained perimeter Gamma limit)E301EA40DFEE

Corollary 16.4.8 (The unconstrained perimeter Gamma limit).E301EA40DFEE On a bounded Lipschitz domain, the energies of Definition 16.2.1 Gamma-converge in $L^1(U)$ to

\[ F_0(u)= \begin{cases} c_ WP(E;U),& u=2\chi _ E-1\text{ a.e.},\ E\text{ of finite perimeter},\\ +\infty ,& \text{otherwise}. \end{cases} \]
Proof

Theorem 16.3.4 is the lower-bound condition. For finite $F_0$, Theorem 16.4.7 is the upper-bound condition. If $F_0(u)=+\infty $, the upper-bound condition is vacuous, and the constant family $u_\varepsilon =u$ in the extended functional domain suffices. This proves the assertion using the explicit external recovery input; it is not a proof of that approximation theorem. Fixed traces or volume constraints would define different admissible problems and require their own recovery arguments. $\square$
