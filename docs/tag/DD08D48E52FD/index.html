Theorem 13.3.4 (Infinitely many minimal hypersurfaces)DD08D48E52FD

Theorem 13.3.4 (Infinitely many minimal hypersurfaces).DD08D48E52FD Every smooth closed connected Riemannian manifold $M^{n+1}$ with $2\leq n\leq 6$ contains infinitely many geometrically distinct smooth closed embedded minimal hypersurfaces. Neither bumpiness nor positive Ricci curvature is required. Source: [Song18], Theorem 1, in the supplied June 22, 2018 version. The statement concerns distinct hypersurfaces, not repeated integer multiples of one surface. Its proof remains external.
