Proposition 15.3.10 (Strong instability under nonnegative Ricci curvature)6BAA97140F05

Proposition 15.3.10 (Strong instability under nonnegative Ricci curvature).6BAA97140F05 In an ambient manifold with $\operatorname {Ric}_ M\geq 0$, every nonempty closed two-sided CMC hypersurface with $c\ne 0$ is unstable for unrestricted $\mathcal A^ c$ variations.

Proof

The pointwise Cauchy–Schwarz inequality gives $|A|^2\geq H^2/n=c^2/n$. Therefore $Q(1,1)=-\int (|A|^2+\operatorname {Ric}(\nu ,\nu ))<0$. The constant speed changes volume, so it is not a permitted test for volume-constrained stability. This is the CMC counterpart of Corollary 11.1.5. $\square$
