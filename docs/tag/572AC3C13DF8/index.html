Theorem 14.4.4 (The Li–Yau multiplicity bound for smooth immersions)572AC3C13DF8

Theorem 14.4.4 (The Li–Yau multiplicity bound for smooth immersions).572AC3C13DF8 For a smooth closed immersion $F:M^2\to \mathbb R^ N$ and any $y\in \mathbb R^ N$,

\[ \mathcal W(F)\geq 4\pi \, \# F^{-1}(y). \]

In particular $\mathcal W(F)\geq 4\pi $ for every nonempty domain, and $\mathcal W(F)<8\pi $ implies that $F$ is an embedding. The multiplicity inequality is [LY82], Theorem 6, p. 286.

Proof

Drop the nonnegative square integral in Theorem 14.4.3. If the domain is nonempty, choose a point of its image to obtain the first universal lower bound. If the energy is less than $8\pi $, no two distinct parameter points can have the same image, so $F$ is injective. A continuous injective map from a compact space to a Hausdorff space is a homeomorphism onto its image. Together with the immersion hypothesis and its local smooth charts, this makes $F$ a smooth embedding. The energy threshold is strict; equality at $8\pi $ does not yield injectivity by this argument. $\square$
