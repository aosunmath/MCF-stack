Corollary 16.3.6 (Energy convergence forces equipartition)194D96DE584A

Corollary 16.3.6 (Energy convergence forces equipartition).194D96DE584A Suppose $u_ j\to u_ E$ in $L^1(U)$, $\varepsilon _ j\downarrow 0$, and $E_{\varepsilon _ j}(u_ j;U)\to c_ WP(E;U)<\infty $. Then

\[ \int _ U|D\Phi (u_ j)|\to c_ WP(E;U),\qquad |\xi _{\varepsilon _ j}^{u_ j}|(U)\to 0. \]

Each of the gradient and potential energies tends to $c_ WP(E;U)/2$.

Proof

For every subsequence, bounded energy gives a further subsequence whose primitives converge locally to $\Phi (u_ E)$. Lower semicontinuity gives the lower bound $c_ WP(E;U)$ for their variations, while the energy inequality gives the matching upper bound. The subsequence criterion therefore gives convergence along the whole sequence. Its energy defect tends to zero. Proposition 16.2.11 makes the total variation of discrepancy tend to zero. Since the sum of the two energies converges to $c_ WP(E;U)$ and their difference tends to zero, each converges to half the sum. $\square$
