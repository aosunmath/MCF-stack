Theorem 8.13.6 (Countable rectifiability of the reduced boundary)C02EADB4998C

Theorem 8.13.6 (Countable rectifiability of the reduced boundary).C02EADB4998C If $E$ has locally finite perimeter in $U\subset \mathbb R^ n$, $n\geq 2$, then $\partial ^*E\cap U$ is contained in a countable union of Lipschitz graphs over $(n-1)$-planes. In particular it is countably $(n-1)$-rectifiable. This proves the graph-cover conclusion in [Sim18], Chapter 3, Theorem 4.4, p. 100. The equality $|D\chi _ E|=\mathcal H^{n-1}\llcorner \partial ^*E$ is not used in this proof.

Proof

Take $\mu =|D\chi _ E|$, $S=\partial ^*E\cap U$, and $e_ x=\nu _ E(x)$. Corollary 8.12.4 gives $\mu (B_ r(x))/r^{n-1}\to \omega _{n-1}>0$ at every point of $S$. Corollary 8.12.5 gives the required cone limit for $\alpha =1/4$. Apply Theorem 8.13.5. Lemma 8.13.2 supplies Hausdorff measurability. The graph map $z\mapsto z+h(z)e$ is Lipschitz, so these are also countably many Lipschitz images in the sense of Definition 7.1.1. The proof covers every reduced-boundary point, rather than introducing an unexamined exceptional subset. Identification of the boundary measure remains a separate step in the full structure theorem. $\square$
