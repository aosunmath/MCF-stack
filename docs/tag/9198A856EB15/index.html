Example 10.6.6 (A stationary varifold with zero density on its support)9198A856EB15

Example 10.6.6 (A stationary varifold with zero density on its support).9198A856EB15 Fix an $m$-plane $P\subset \mathbb R^ N$, $m<N$. The general varifold $V=\mathcal L^ N\otimes \delta _ P$ is stationary, has full support, and has zero $m$-density at every point.

Proof

In coordinates adapted to $P$, first variation is the integral over $\mathbb R^ N$ of $\sum _{i=1}^ m\partial _ iX_ i$, which vanishes by Fubini and compact support. Its normalized mass ratio is $(\omega _ N/\omega _ m)r^{N-m}$, tending to zero. Its weight is not $m$-rectifiable. Thus Corollary 10.3.4 cannot be extended to arbitrary stationary varifolds. $\square$
