Definition 12.5.1 (A mass cap and its stationary subset)7FF786CD9267

Definition 12.5.1 (A mass cap and its stationary subset).7FF786CD9267 For $C>0$ let $K_ C$ be the space of all nonnegative $n$-varifolds $V$ on $M$ with $\| V\| (M)\leq C$, equipped with the bounded-Lipschitz metric $\mathbf F_ V$ on $G_ n(M)$. Write

\[ S_ C=\{ V\in K_ C:\delta V(X)=0\text{ for every smooth vector field }X\} . \]

Thus $0\in S_ C$. This compact ambient space, rather than a space restricted to integral varifolds, is used in [LiAP], Section 4.2. The first variation is $\delta V(X)=\int \operatorname {div}_ P X\, dV(x,P)$.
