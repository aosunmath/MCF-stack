Example 8.7.5 (A diffuse stationary varifold on a compact manifold)D95BB0100791

Example 8.7.5 (A diffuse stationary varifold on a compact manifold).D95BB0100791 For $n\geq 1$ on the unit flat torus $M=\mathbb R^{n+1}/\mathbb Z^{n+1}$, let $P$ be the parallel plane spanned by the first $n$ coordinate fields. The varifold defined by

\[ \int f\, dV=\int _ M f(x,P)\, d\operatorname {Vol}_{n+1}(x) \]

is stationary, has positive finite mass, and is not $n$-rectifiable.

Proof

For a smooth periodic vector field $X$, its first variation is $\int _ M\sum _{i=1}^ n\partial _ iX_ i\, dx=0$ by integration over each periodic coordinate. Its weight is ambient volume. Any countably $n$-rectifiable set has zero $(n+1)$-dimensional volume: each bounded piece of a Lipschitz image of a subset of $\mathbb R^ n$ has zero ambient volume by the elementary dimension estimate, and countable unions retain this property. Thus ambient volume cannot be carried by such a set. This example shows why the compact mass cap in the deformation argument cannot silently be treated as a compact space of integral varifolds. $\square$
