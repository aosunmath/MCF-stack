Theorem 14.4.3 (A square identity for a compact immersed surface)A6413522A6DE

Theorem 14.4.3 (A square identity for a compact immersed surface).A6413522A6DE Let $F:M^2\to \mathbb R^ N$ be a smooth compact immersion with smooth boundary allowed. For $y\notin F(\partial M)$ let $m=\# F^{-1}(y)$, and let $\eta $ be the outward unit conormal on the original boundary. Then

\[ \pi m+\int _{M\setminus F^{-1}(y)} \left|\frac{\vec H}{4}+\frac{(F-y)^\perp }{|F-y|^2}\right|^2d\mu =\frac{\mathcal W(F)}4+ \frac12\int _{\partial M}\frac{(F-y)\cdot \eta }{|F-y|^2}\, ds. \]

The boundary integral is zero for a closed surface. All integrals retain parameter multiplicity. This is the smooth immersed form of the identity used in [SimW93], equation (1.5).

Proof

On the punctured surface, set $V=X/r^2$. Ambient tangential divergence gives

\[ \operatorname {div}_ M V=\frac2{r^2}-2\frac{|X^ T|^2}{r^4} =2\frac{|X^\perp |^2}{r^4}. \]

The intrinsic divergence of $V^ T$ is $\operatorname {div}_ MV+\vec H\cdot V$. To see the sign, for an orthonormal tangent frame, differentiating $\langle V^\perp ,e_ i\rangle =0$ gives $\operatorname {div}_ MV^\perp =-\vec H\cdot V$. Therefore

\[ \operatorname {div}_ M(V^ T) =2\left|\frac{\vec H}{4}+\frac{X^\perp }{r^2}\right|^2-\frac18|\vec H|^2. \]

Integrate over the surface with the small sheet disks removed. The original boundary contributes the displayed flux, and Lemma 14.4.2 gives the limiting inner flux $-2\pi m$. The same lemma and smoothness of $\vec H$ give integrability of both terms near each puncture. Letting the radii tend to zero and rearranging proves the identity. Smoothness of the image as a set at a multiple point is not required: each parameter sheet has its own puncture. $\square$
