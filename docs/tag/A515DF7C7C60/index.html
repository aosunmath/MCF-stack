Example 2.11.1 (Kapouleas–Kleene–Moller shrinkers with one end)A515DF7C7C60

Example 2.11.1 (Kapouleas–Kleene–Moller shrinkers with one end).A515DF7C7C60 There is an integer $g_0$ such that for every integer $g\geq g_0$ there exists a smooth complete embedded orientable self-shrinker $\Sigma _ g\subset \mathbb R^3$ of genus $g$, with

\[ \vec H=-\frac12X^\perp ,\qquad M_ t=\sqrt{-t}\, \Sigma _ g,\quad -\infty <t<0. \]

The construction desingularizes the intersection circle of a plane through the origin and $S^2(2)$. It is a gluing existence theorem; no explicit parametrization of $\Sigma _ g$ is provided.

  • The surface has dimension two and codimension one, is noncompact, has exactly one end, and separates $\mathbb R^3$ into two connected components. Its topology is an orientable closed genus-$g$ surface with one point removed. The compact core and graphical end give properness.

  • It is invariant under the dihedral symmetry group of order $4g+4$ specified in the construction.

  • Outside a ball the end is a graph over a plane, asymptotic to a nonplanar regular cone whose link is a nonzero smooth vertical graph over a great circle of $S^2$. The end has asymptotic height $u(\rho ,\theta )=\rho \sigma (\theta )+O(\rho ^{-1})$; the function $\sigma $ is determined by the construction, not an explicit formula. See equation (1.2), PDF p. 4.

  • As $g\to \infty $, the surfaces converge locally in Hausdorff distance to $S^2(2)\cup P$. The source gives an $O(R/g)$ Hausdorff bound inside $B_ R(0)$; convergence is locally smooth away from the intersection circle. The limiting union is singular, and is not one of the smooth shrinkers.

  • The conical geometry gives quadratic area growth and finite entropy. The source supplies no exact entropy value or numerical entropy bound for individual $g$. It records entropy instability on PDF p. 4. No uniqueness or identification with another construction is asserted.

The illustration shows a labelled sphere–plane gluing configuration and a nonplanar conical end schematic. A handle diagram or a generic wavy cone is not a numerically solved $\Sigma _ g$.
