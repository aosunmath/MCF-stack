Corollary 7.13.9 (A null image is equivalent to a vanishing area Jacobian)C5AE6A4945BD

Corollary 7.13.9 (A null image is equivalent to a vanishing area Jacobian).C5AE6A4945BD Under Theorem 7.13.8, $\mathcal H^ m(f(A))=0$ if and only if $J_ mDf=0$ almost everywhere on $A$. In particular, a measurable positive-volume set on which the area Jacobian is positive almost everywhere has an image of positive $m$-dimensional measure.

Proof

The image is measurable by countable local versions of Lemma 7.13.2. Its indicator is bounded above by $N_ A$, since every point of the image has at least one preimage. If the Jacobian vanishes almost everywhere, the area identity gives $\int N_ A=0$ and hence zero measure for the image. Conversely if the image is null, $N_ A$ vanishes off a null set, so its integral and the Jacobian integral are zero. Nonnegativity of the Jacobian gives its almost-everywhere vanishing. The final assertion is the contrapositive with a positive integral, which may be infinite. $\square$
