Example 2.9.1 (Higher-codimension spherical shrinkers)45597E4891F4

Example 2.9.1 (Higher-codimension spherical shrinkers).45597E4891F4 Let $n\geq 2$ and let $F:M^ n\to S^{N-1}(\sqrt{2n})$ be a smooth compact minimal immersion. Viewed in $\mathbb R^ N$, it satisfies

\[ \vec H=-\frac12F, \qquad F_ t=\sqrt{-t}\, F,\quad -\infty <t<0. \]

An explicit family is obtained for integers $p,q\geq 1$, $n=p+q$, by

\[ F(u,v)=(\sqrt{2p}\, u,\sqrt{2q}\, v), \quad (u,v)\in S^ p(1)\times S^ q(1), \quad N=p+q+2. \]

To avoid repeating the Clifford torus entry, one may take $p+q\geq 3$ in this explicit family.

  • For a general compact minimal immersion, the resulting shrinker is complete and proper; it is embedded when the original immersion is embedded. Topology, symmetry and further curvature properties depend on the chosen minimal submanifold and are not fixed by this construction.

  • Its mean-curvature vector has constant length $\sqrt{n/2}$, and the principal normal $\vec H/|\vec H|$ is parallel in the normal bundle. Conversely, a compact self-shrinker of dimension $n\geq 2$ is spherical if and only if its mean-curvature vector is nowhere zero and its principal normal is parallel (Smoczyk's Theorem 1.1).

  • The displayed product family is compact embedded of Euclidean codimension two, with topology $S^ p\times S^ q$ and $O(p+1)\times O(q+1)$ symmetry. It has $|A|^2=1$ and is minimal inside $S^{n+1}(\sqrt{2n})$; these identities follow directly from its two round factors.

The formula determines the product geometry exactly. The figure is a factor diagram with the full ambient dimension stated.
