Theorem 18.23.2 (The localized reciprocal-angle estimate)60A54E588296

Theorem 18.23.2 (The localized reciprocal-angle estimate).60A54E588296 For a smooth entire graphical flow on $[0,T]$, every ambient center and every $R>0$ satisfy

\[ v(x,t)\bigl (R^2-|F(x,t)-X_0|^2-2nt\bigr )_+ \leq \sup _ y v(y,0)\bigl (R^2-|F(y,0)-X_0|^2\bigr )_+. \]

The right side is finite by compactness of the base support. This is [EH91], Theorem 2.1, p. 550, for an entire graph. No global curvature or slope bound is assumed.

Proof

On $\eta >0$ put $q=v\eta $. The angle equation and Lemma 18.23.1 give

\[ \left(\mathcal P+2v^{-1}\nabla v\cdot \nabla \right)q=-a q\leq 0. \]

Indeed the two cross-gradient terms and the term $-2\eta v^{-1}|\nabla v|^2$ cancel after adding this drift. The function $v\eta _+$ is continuous and vanishes at the spatial boundary of its support. All of that support lies in a compact base set over a finite time interval. On its interior every coefficient is smooth and bounded. The graph-coordinate drift from Lemma 18.18.3 vanishes at a spatial maximum, so the usual first-positive-maximum argument applies to $v\eta _+-M-\epsilon t$, where $M$ is its initial supremum. A positive maximum cannot occur on $\eta =0$. Let $\epsilon \downarrow 0$. Only the positive region is differentiated; no smoothness of the positive-part function across its zero set is asserted. $\square$
