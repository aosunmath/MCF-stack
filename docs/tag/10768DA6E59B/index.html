Proposition 6.5.3 (The weak maximal estimate for finite measures)10768DA6E59B

Proposition 6.5.3 (The weak maximal estimate for finite measures).10768DA6E59B If $\nu $ is a finite nonnegative Radon measure on $\mathbb R^ n$ and $\lambda >0$, then

\[ \mathcal L^ n(\{ M\nu >\lambda \} )\leq \frac{5^ n}{\lambda }\nu (\mathbb R^ n). \]

In particular $M\nu <\infty $ almost everywhere for Lebesgue measure. The constant is not claimed to be sharp.

Proof

For each $x\in \{ M\nu >\lambda \} \cap B_ R(0)$ choose a ball centered at $x$ with $\nu (B_ r(x))>\lambda \omega _ n r^ n$. Its radius is bounded in terms of $\nu (\mathbb R^ n)$ and $\lambda $. Lemma 6.5.1 gives disjoint selected balls covering the set after fivefold dilation. Therefore its Lebesgue measure is at most

\[ 5^ n\sum _ j\omega _ n r_ j^ n \leq \frac{5^ n}{\lambda }\sum _ j\nu (B_{r_ j}(x_ j)) \leq \frac{5^ n}{\lambda }\nu (\mathbb R^ n). \]

Let $R\to \infty $. Letting $\lambda \to \infty $ then shows that the infinite-value set is null. If the measure is zero, the assertions are immediate. This is the maximal-estimate application of the covering principle in [Sim18], Lemma 3.4. $\square$
