§ 4.12 Reported multiphase expanding surface constructionsBEE2570F7E0E

BEE2570F7E0E
55F8A309578D

Liao's 2025 preprint proposes self-expanding multiphase surfaces in $\mathbb R^3$ with regular triple curves and tetrahedral quadruple points, obtained from weighted-area minimization with prescribed asymptotic interface data. Triple sheets meet at $120$ degrees; four triple curves have the tetrahedral angle $\arccos (-1/3)$. The intended flow is $\sqrt t\, \Gamma $, $t>0$, sheetwise away from junctions. This entry records a reported construction, not a verified existence theorem of this catalogue. The compactness, persistence of junctions and conformal comparison used in Sections 3–5 require further checking. In particular one must distinguish the expander-weighted Euclidean metric from the hyperbolic metric; a conformal comparison does not automatically transport minimality or area minimization. Accordingly no definite global topology, embeddedness, completeness, uniqueness, or entropy assertion is assigned here. The tetrahedral junction drawing illustrates the local angle model only; that stationary cone by itself is not a new nontrivial self-expander. The panels are explicitly labeled proposed smoothing stages, rather than computed or proved flow slices.

Sources: [Liao2025].
