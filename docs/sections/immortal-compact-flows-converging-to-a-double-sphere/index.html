5.8 Immortal compact flows converging to a double sphere0806DD487DC4

0806DD487DC4
225751B3C7E8

(Chen–Sun's product-manifold construction) For each $n\geq 2$, there is a smooth connected embedded immortal mean curvature flow of compact hypersurfaces in the product manifold

\[ (S^ n\times [-1,1],\ g_{S^ n}+dy^2), \]

where $S^ n$ has unit round radius. The flow is defined for all $t\geq 0$ and is nonstatic. Its hypersurface dimension is $n$ and its codimension in this ambient manifold is one. The construction stays in the smaller band $|y|\leq 1/(2n)$, so the same flow is contained in $S^ n\times \mathbb R$; it can also be placed in the closed product $S^ n\times (\mathbb R/4\mathbb Z)$ without changing the metric near the flow. There is no free boundary on $y=\pm 1$.

The solution is invariant under rotations of the sphere about its polar axis, reflection $y\mapsto -y$, and reflection across the sphere's equator. A closed profile $\sigma \mapsto (x(\sigma ,t),y(\sigma ,t))$ in $(0,\pi )\times \mathbb R$ generates it by

\[ (\sigma ,\omega )\longmapsto (\cos x(\sigma ,t),\sin x(\sigma ,t)\, \omega ,y(\sigma ,t)), \qquad \sigma \in S^1,\quad \omega \in S^{n-1}. \]

The profile avoids both axes at every finite time, so this parametrization directly gives topology $S^1\times S^{n-1}$. Thus for $n=2$ the surfaces are genus-one tori with no ends. All slices are complete and proper by compactness. This is a topological deduction from the construction, not an assertion that the source states a separate topology theorem.

No closed time-dependent profile is supplied. Its upper half can be described using the solution of

\[ v_ t=\frac{v_{xx}}{1+v_ x^2}+(n-1)\cot x\, v_ x, \qquad v_ x(\pi /2,t)=0, \]

with an inverse graph near each neck, governed by

\[ u_ t=\frac{u_{yy}}{1+u_ y^2}-(n-1)\cot u, \qquad u_ y(0,t)=0. \]

An interpolation argument selects an immortal solution. As $t\to \infty $, it converges to the stable minimal slice $S^ n\times \{ 0\} $ with multiplicity two. Away from the north and south poles, the two sheets converge smoothly; the two necks collapse only at infinite time.

Source: [CS25Man], Theorems 1.1, 3.12, and 3.17, Section 2.1, Definition 3.10 and Lemma 3.11, and Section 3.3, arXiv v2 PDF pp. 1–4 and 11–15. Euclidean Gaussian area and Colding–Minicozzi entropy are not assigned to this intrinsic manifold flow; the cited paper gives no such invariant for these examples. In particular, its isometric embedding into $\mathbb R^{n+2}$ is not an unforced Euclidean mean curvature flow. The picture shows the orbit-space profile and the limiting minimal slice schematically; it does not represent an isometric embedding of $S^2\times \mathbb R$ into three-dimensional Euclidean space.

Ambient spaces: Curved ambient spaces
Ambient space, topology, embeddedness and entropy

The flow stays in a narrow product band and also fits in a closed Sⁿ × S¹ product. Its Euclidean embedding is not an unforced Euclidean MCF.
