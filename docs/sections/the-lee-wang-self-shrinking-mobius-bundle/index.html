§ 2.44 The Lee–Wang self-shrinking Mobius bundle5D9025FCD9CD

5D9025FCD9CD This example is a surface in $\mathbb C^2=\mathbb R^4$, of codimension two.
CC2E5DD6BD78

(An embedded nonorientable shrinker) Let $M=(\mathbb S^1\times \mathbb R)/((\theta ,r)\sim (\theta +\pi ,-r))$ and set

\[ X([\theta ,r])=(e^{2i\theta }\cosh r,\sqrt2 e^{-i\theta }\sinh r). \]

This is an exact parametrization with $\vec H=-X^\perp /2$; its ancient flow is $\sqrt{-t}\, X(M)$, $t<0$. Lee–Wang constructed the surface, up to a unitary change of coordinates, in [LeeWang2009], Section 2.2. The quotient map is an embedding; the map on $\mathbb S^1\times \mathbb R$ is a double cover, not an embedding.

The surface is Lagrangian, Hamiltonian stationary, noncompact, and asymptotic to a Schoen–Wolfson $(2,1)$ cone. Its topology is the open Mobius line bundle, with one end and one crosscap. Orientable surface genus does not apply. The identity $|X|^2=1+3\sinh ^2r$ proves properness, and the induced metric

\[ g=(\sinh ^2r+2\cosh ^2r)(2\, d\theta ^2+dr^2) \]

proves completeness. The diagonal circle action has weights $(2,-1)$.

For this single-covered embedding, [BraxtonLeeZhu2026], Theorem 1.2 and Section 7, prove $F$-stability under arbitrary normal variations modulo translations and dilation. This does not establish nonlinear stability or realization as a generic singularity. Remark 7.4 gives

\[ F_{0,1}(M)=\lambda (M)=\frac{\sqrt2 e^{1/8}}8 \left[K_0(3/8)+3K_1(3/8)\right],\qquad K_ j(z)=\int _0^\infty e^{-z\cosh v}\cosh (jv)\, dv. \]

The illustration projects the exact four-dimensional parametrization; projected crossings are not self-intersections of $M$.
