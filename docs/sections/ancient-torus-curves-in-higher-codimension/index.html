§ 5.12 Ancient torus curves in higher codimensionB6082DA55C46

B6082DA55C46
D28FAF32B556

(Compact ancient torus curves) Fix $m\geq 2$ and integers $1=k_1<k_2<\cdots <k_ m$. For $t<0$, let $r(t)>0$ be the unique solution of

\[ \sum _{j=1}^ m r(t)^{2k_ j^2}=-2t. \]

Then the exact parametrization

\[ \gamma _ t(\theta )= \bigl (r(t)^{k_1^2}\cos (k_1\theta ),r(t)^{k_1^2}\sin (k_1\theta ), \ldots ,r(t)^{k_ m^2}\cos (k_ m\theta ),r(t)^{k_ m^2}\sin (k_ m\theta )\bigr ), \qquad \theta \in \mathbb R/2\pi \mathbb Z, \]

is an ancient curve-shortening flow in $\mathbb R^{2m}$. Its scale is specified by an exact implicit equation; no elementary inverse in time is required. Equivalently,

\[ r'=-\frac{r}{\sum _ j k_ j^2r^{2k_ j^2}}. \]

Each time slice is a compact smooth embedded circle, hence complete and proper, with no ends and codimension $2m-1$. Embeddedness follows directly from $k_1=1$, because the first two coordinates already determine $\theta $. The name refers to a curve lying on a product of circles, not to a two-dimensional torus. It spans $\mathbb R^{2m}$, has a one-parameter simultaneous rotational symmetry, and is non-self-similar when $m\geq 2$ because the ratios of its different coordinate radii change with time.

The rescaled curves $(-t)^{-1/2}\gamma _ t$ tend to the radius-$\sqrt2$ circle with multiplicity $k_ m$ as $t\to -\infty $, and with multiplicity one as $t\uparrow 0$. Stryker–Sun prove

\[ \sup _{t<0}\lambda (\gamma _ t)=k_ m\lambda (S^1) =k_ m\sqrt{\frac{2\pi }{e}}, \]

which also bounds the entropy of every time slice. This is a supremum over time, not a formula for the entropy at each finite time. For the fixed center and Gaussian scale, direct integration gives

\[ F_{0,1}(\gamma _ t)=\sqrt\pi \left(\sum _ j k_ j^2r^{2k_ j^2}\right)^{1/2}e^{t/2}. \]

Indeed, $|\gamma _ t|^2=-2t$ and $|\partial _\theta \gamma _ t|^2=\sum _ jk_ j^2r^{2k_ j^2}$. The centered Gaussian area is distinct from the supremum defining $\lambda $.

Source: [SS19], equation (2.1), Propositions 2.1–2.2, Corollary 2.3, and Theorem A.1, PDF pp. 3–5 and 8–9. The general source formula allows $k_1>1$; if the frequencies have common divisor $d>1$, that parametrization is a $d$-fold cover and its mass counts multiplicity. The embedded subfamily above avoids this ambiguity. The construction was already present in [AAAW13], Section 3, equations (11)–(14), PDF pp. 8–9, as acknowledged in [SS19], Remark 1.3. The interactive figure uses $(k_1,k_2)=(1,2)$ in $\mathbb R^4$. Its three-coordinate display is a projection, with time and fourth-coordinate rotation controls.
