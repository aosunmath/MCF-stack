§ 5.38 Lagrangian singularities with prescribed orbit modelsA3EE122FF71E

A3EE122FF71E
5DA3E72E4195

Let $G\subset SU(n)$ be an admissible compact connected group, and let $L_\infty \subset \mu ^{-1}(0)\subset \mathbb C^ n$ be a complete connected cohomogeneity-one special Lagrangian. Here each orbit has dimension $n-1$ and the same isotropy type. Madnick–Wood construct an almost-calibrated Lagrangian MCF developing a Type II singularity at the origin for which every Type II blow-up is $L_\infty $ and every Type I blow-up is its asymptotic cone. The profile flow in a complex line is

\[ (\partial _ t\gamma )^\perp =\mathbf k-(n-1)\frac{\gamma ^\perp }{|\gamma |^2}. \]

It is not ordinary planar curve shortening. Admissible non-$SO(n)$ groups, for example the diagonal $T^{n-1}$ action, give models beyond the currently listed rotational Lawlor neck. The theorem concerns the evolving singularity-forming flow; its stationary special Lagrangian limit is not counted as an additional moving soliton. Smoothness is restricted to the source's constant-isotropy stratum. We do not infer full embeddedness or topology solely from a projected profile. The illustration uses an exact model profile with a schematic neck scale, clearly distinguished from the nonlinear flow constructed in the proof.

Sources: [MadnickWood2025].
