§ 5.39 Type-II smoothing after a Simons-cone singularity (Angenent–Daskalopoulos–Šešum, 2021)AA223119E79B

Paper credits and chronology

Title credits include all authors of papers with a reviewed construction of this example, in chronological order. Later analysis, surveys, and citations remain listed here; they do not by themselves earn construction credit. Alternative and numerical constructions are qualified below. These credits are not an exhaustive discovery history. Background-only citations do not determine the date of this example. A classical example may predate the references recorded here.

  1. Angenent–Daskalopoulos–Šešum — 2021-08-19 (first arXiv submission). Construction: Proves post-singularity smoothing and bounded mean curvature; credits formal matching to Angenent–Ilmanen–Velázquez. Passage: Main Theorem and Corollary 1.1.1; Sections 3–5.
AA223119E79B
0B7679E73801

(Angenent–Daskalopoulos–Šešum continuation) In $\mathbb R^8=\mathbb R^4\times \mathbb R^4$, let $M_0$ be the $O(4)\times O(4)$ invariant singular hypersurface with profile $u_0(r)$, smooth for $r>0$, such that for an integer $k\geq 4$ and $K_0>0$,

\[ u_0(r)=r+K_0r^{2(k-1)}+o(r^{2(k-1)})\quad (r\downarrow 0). \]

Retain the source's global hypotheses $0\leq u_0'\leq C_0$, $|u_0''|\leq C_0$ and $|u_0''(r)|\leq C_0r^{2k-4}$, and, after rescaling, $u_0'\geq c>0$ on $(0,1]$. Angenent–Daskalopoulos–Šešum produce a smooth positive profile $u(r,t)$ for $0<t\leq t_0$ satisfying

\[ u_ t=\frac{u_{rr}}{1+u_ r^2}+\frac3r u_ r-\frac3u, \qquad u_ r(0,t)=0,\qquad \lim _{t\downarrow 0}u(r,t)=u_0(r). \]

Its hypersurfaces are smooth complete noncompact embedded seven-manifolds, of codimension one, with uniformly bounded $|H|$ for sufficiently small positive times. The positive radial graph gives properness, topology $\mathbb R^4\times S^3$, and one end. The initial vertex remains a singular point of $M_0$; its mean curvature is not assigned a classical value there.

The two relevant spatial scales are $\sqrt t$ and $t^{k/3}$. The inner expansion has the leading form

\[ u(r,t)\sim t^{k/3}K_2W\left(\frac{r}{K_2t^{k/3}}\right),\qquad \frac{W''}{1+(W')^2}+\frac3zW'-\frac3W=0, \]

where $W(z)=z+z^{-2}+o(z^{-2})$ at infinity and $K_2^3=K_0(2k+1)!!$. The scale and normalization come from the matching calculation in Section 2. This is an asymptotic model, not an exact evolving dilation of a minimal hypersurface. The solution is not a self-expander.

For compatible incoming Velázquez–Stolarski data with even $k\geq 4$, the source obtains a flow on $(-t_0,t_0)$ which is smooth away from the single spacetime point $(0,0)$ and has uniformly bounded mean curvature on its regular slices, while $|A|$ blows up near that point. This does not mean a smooth classical flow through time zero, and it does not establish uniqueness of continuation. The even-mode restriction concerns the incoming bounded-curvature theorem; the outgoing construction allows all integer $k\geq 4$.

Source: [ADS2026], equations (1.1.1)–(1.1.5), Main Theorem and Corollary 1.1.1, printed pp. 858–860; Sections 2–5, especially (2.3.2)–(2.3.6), pp. 862–863. The paper credits formal continuation asymptotics to Angenent–Ilmanen–Velázquez; the proved continuation here is the Angenent–Daskalopoulos–Šešum construction. No Gaussian-area or entropy value or dynamical stability assertion is recorded.

The interactive experiment solves the nonlinear reduced PDE on $0\leq r\leq 5$ for a regularization of $u_0(r)=r+0.2r^6/(1+r^6)$, with a smooth axis and a fixed outer boundary. This $k=4$ trace satisfies the displayed asymptotic and derivative hypotheses. The initial rounding replaces $r$ by $\varepsilon P(r/\varepsilon )$ with $P(0)=1$, and is explicitly varied in the numerical audit. Positive rounding and finite boundary are numerical approximations, not the singular initial-value problem itself. Mesh, domain and rounding comparisons are supplied; they do not prove convergence to the theorem's solution or certify its blow-up rate. See also 5.36.
