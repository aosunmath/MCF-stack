§ 4.5 One-sheeted expanders of rotational double cones40FCBC562153

40FCBC562153
3A4775A9BD2C

(Connected double-cone smoothings) For $n\geq 2$ and a neck parameter $a>0$, the initial value problem

\[ u''=(1+(u')^2)\left(\frac{n-1}{u}+\frac{u-zu'}2\right), \qquad u(0)=a,\quad u'(0)=0, \]

defines a positive, smooth, even profile on $\mathbb R$. Rotating it gives

\[ M_ a=\{ (u(z)\omega ,z):z\in \mathbb R,\ \omega \in S^{n-1}\} \subset \mathbb R^{n+1}. \]

This is a smooth complete properly embedded noncompact expander with topology $S^{n-1}\times \mathbb R$, rotational symmetry, reflection symmetry, and two conical ends. The profile is increasing for $z>0$. Its limiting aperture depends on $a$; the formula is an ODE specification, not an elementary expression. The evolution is $\sqrt t\, M_ a$ for $t>0$.

For every $\alpha >\alpha _{\rm crit}(n+1)$ there exists such a connected expander asymptotic to $D_\alpha $ from Example 4.4. It gives a second thin evolution from that cone alongside the disconnected two-sheeted solution. The cited theorem uses a strict inequality and does not establish a universal uniqueness assertion for the one-sheeted profile. Source: [AIC95], Lemma 2(ii), Lemma 3, and Theorem 4, printed pp. 1947–1951, supplied PDF pp. 12–16. These qualitative expander statements are proved in the source; its separate computed higher-genus shrinker and numerical threshold estimates are not needed here. The interactive figure shows the numerical solution in $\mathbb R^3$ with $a=0.8$ on a finite axial interval.
