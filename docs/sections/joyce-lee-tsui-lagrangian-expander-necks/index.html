§ 4.10 Joyce–Lee–Tsui Lagrangian expander necks6095D0C72380

6095D0C72380 These expanders smooth two transverse Lagrangian planes in $\mathbb C^ n$.
E25A2DDDEA95

(Expanding Lagrangian necks) Fix $n\geq 2$, $a_ j>0$, and $\psi _ j\in \mathbb R$. Set

\[ P_\alpha (y)=\frac{e^{\alpha y^2}\prod _{j=1}^ n(1+a_ jy^2)-1}{y^2}, \quad P_\alpha (0)=\alpha +\sum _ ja_ j,\quad \phi _ j(y)=\psi _ j+\int _0^ y\frac{dt}{(a_ j^{-1}+t^2)\sqrt{P_\alpha (t)}}. \]

With $\alpha =1/2$, the exact integral parametrization

\[ X(x,y)=\left(x_ j\sqrt{a_ j^{-1}+y^2}\, e^{i\phi _ j(y)}\right)_{j=1}^ n, \qquad (x,y)\in \mathbb S^{n-1}\times \mathbb R, \]

satisfies $\vec H=X^\perp /2$ and gives the flow $\sqrt t\, X$, $t>0$. By [JoyceLeeTsui2010], Theorems C–D, its image is a proper embedded Lagrangian neck. It is complete, has dimension and codimension $n$, and has two ends. For $n=2$ its topology is an annulus; generally it is $\mathbb S^{n-1}\times \mathbb R$.

Write $\bar\phi _ j=\phi _ j(+\infty )-\psi _ j$. The limiting planes have coordinate phases $\psi _ j\pm \bar\phi _ j$, with $0<2\sum _ j\bar\phi _ j<\pi $. Every admissible angle tuple determines unique positive $a_ j$ at fixed $\alpha $ within this family. The neck is zero-Maslov and almost calibrated. Equal $a_ j$ and equal $\psi _ j$ give rotational symmetry under $\mathrm{SO}(n)$.

For $n\geq 3$, the stronger uniqueness theorem in [ImagiJoyceOliveira2016], Theorem 1.2, requires an embedded, exact, closed-as-a-subset Lagrangian expander asymptotic to two transverse planes at rate $\rho <2$. These hypotheses must not be dropped. No general stability claim is attached. The figure projects the $n=2$, $a_1=a_2=1$, $\psi _1=\psi _2=0$ member, evaluating its integrals numerically.
