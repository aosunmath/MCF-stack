3.7 Scherkenoid translators9E847D647A0C

9E847D647A0C

Example 3.7.1 (Scherkenoid translators).C6C5A44F27CC The supplied notes describe Scherkenoids as complete, properly embedded, nongraphical translators in $\mathbb R^3$, obtained as limits of Scherk-type translators. Their height is not given by an explicit formula.

  • They are singly periodic. The notes describe a one-parameter family using the angle between two asymptotic plane directions; no exhaustive parameterization of all Scherkenoids is asserted here.

  • In the source's downward convention there is one asymptotic plane as $z\to +\infty $ and an infinite family of parallel asymptotic planes as $z\to -\infty $. For the upward convention used in this catalogue these two height directions are reversed.

  • These are noncompact eternal flows. A picture of their asymptotic planes records only end arrangement and periodicity, not an exact or numerically solved Scherkenoid.

See [CatHIMWNotes19], Section 5 and Figure 6, PDF pages 11–12. That supplied edition surveys constructions proved elsewhere; the present entry retains that external-proof status. A later detailed source is [CatHMW20]. No uniqueness, genus, entropy, or stability claim is inferred from the schematic.
