§ 5.36 Hypersurface singularities with bounded mean curvature (Velázquez, 1994)88709443EE99

Paper credits and chronology

Title credits include all authors of papers with a reviewed construction of this example, in chronological order. Later analysis, surveys, and citations remain listed here; they do not by themselves earn construction credit. Alternative and numerical constructions are qualified below. These credits are not an exhaustive discovery history. Background-only citations do not determine the date of this example. A classical example may predate the references recorded here.

  1. Velázquez — 1994 (publication year). Construction: Constructs the degree-selected type-II family; subsequent mean-curvature conclusions are credited separately in the text. Passage: Proposition 2.2, Theorems 2.1–2.2; printed pp. 607–611.
  2. Stolarski — 2020-03-13 (first arXiv submission). Analysis: Proves bounded mean curvature for an existing subfamily and explicitly attributes its construction to Velázquez. Passage: Theorems 1.1 and 3.2; Introduction attribution, PDF pp. 1–2.
88709443EE99
D9D28F3C8E4F

For $n\geq 4$ and each even integer $k\geq 4$, Stolarski proves that a subfamily of Velázquez's $O(n)\times O(n)$ invariant flows in $\mathbb R^{2n}$ develops a type-II singularity at time zero while

\[ \sup _{t_0\leq t<0}\sup _{M_ t}|H|<\infty , \qquad \sup _{M_ t}|A|\asymp (-t)^{-\beta _ k}\longrightarrow \infty , \qquad \beta _ k=\frac{k}{1-\alpha _ n},\quad \alpha _ n=\frac{-(2n-3)+\sqrt{4n^2-20n+17}}2. \]

The time interval is finite. Each regular slice is a complete properly embedded noncompact hypersurface with topology $\mathbb R^ n\times S^{n-1}$ and one end, as follows from the positive radial graph $Q(r,t)$ on $r\geq 0$. It has dimension $2n-1$ and codimension one. Taking a product with $\mathbb R^ m$, $m\geq 1$, gives topology $\mathbb R^{n+m}\times S^{n-1}$ and examples in $\mathbb R^{2n+m}$. This accounts for every ambient dimension $N\geq 8$. In product examples the singular set also acquires the flat factor; it is not an isolated point.

The exact reduced equation, with smooth-axis condition, is

\[ Q_ t=\frac{Q_{rr}}{1+Q_ r^2}+\frac{n-1}{r}Q_ r-\frac{n-1}{Q}, \qquad Q_ r(0,t)=0,\quad Q(0,t)>0. \]

At scale $(-t)^{-1/2}$ the flow approaches the stationary Simons cone away from its vertex. At scale $(-t)^{-\beta _ k}$ it approaches a smooth minimal desingularization locally smoothly. For $n=4$, $\alpha _4=-2$ and $\beta _ k=k/3$. Opposite principal-curvature contributions can cancel in $H$ while $|A|$ grows. Profile convexity is not convexity of the full hypersurface.

Sources: [Velazquez1994], Proposition 2.2 and Theorems 2.1–2.2; [Stolarski2023], Theorems 1.1, 3.1–3.3, equation (2.1) and Sections 6–7 (reviewed arXiv v1 PDF pp. 1–6 and the cited sections). The even $k\geq 4$ restriction is retained exactly for Stolarski's theorem. The low mode $k=2$ with $n\geq 5$ has unbounded mean curvature by Guo–Šešum; see 5.37. A stationary minimal blow-up alone therefore does not imply bounded mean curvature of the original flow.

No closed time-dependent formula, general uniqueness, dynamical stability, or quantitative entropy or centered Gaussian-area value is supplied here. The numerical figure integrates the minimal-profile ODE and displays the two asymptotic magnifications; changing the scale of a minimal leaf is not itself MCF. The theorem is noncompact. Liu's compact extension and the Angenent–Daskalopoulos–Šešum post-singularity smoothing have separate hypotheses and are recorded in 5.41 and 5.39.
