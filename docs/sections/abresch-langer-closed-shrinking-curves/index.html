§ 2.1 Closed planar shrinking curves (Abresch–Langer, 1986; Halldórsson, 2010; Drugan–Lee–X. H. Nguyen, 2017; Colding–Minicozzi II, 2019)B4D1BD157D4B

The closed planar curves are classified by [AL1986]. The supplied sources record the curvature conservation laws in [DLN2018], Theorem 1, PDF pp. 5–6, and the rotation-index description in [CMComplexity2020], Section 6.3, supplied PDF p. 22.

55D5743A6941

Besides the round circle, there are noncircular closed immersed curves $\gamma _{m,\ell }\subset \mathbb R^2$, indexed primitively by coprime positive integers satisfying

\[ \frac12<\frac m\ell <\frac1{\sqrt2}. \]

Here $m$ is the rotation index and $\ell $ is the number of periods of the curvature. Their flows are $\sqrt{-t}\, \gamma _{m,\ell }$ for $t<0$. An ODE description in the normalization of this collection is

\[ \kappa _{\theta \theta }+\kappa =\frac1{2\kappa },\qquad \kappa >0,\qquad \kappa _\theta ^2+\kappa ^2-\log \kappa =E. \]

Choose the nonconstant periodic solution with curvature period $2\pi m/\ell $. With $T(\theta )=(\cos \theta ,\sin \theta )$ and $N(\theta )=(-\sin \theta ,\cos \theta )$, reconstruction gives

\[ X(\theta )=2\kappa _\theta T(\theta )-2\kappa N(\theta ), \qquad \theta \in \mathbb R/(2\pi m\mathbb Z). \]

This is an ODE construction with a closing condition, not a closed elementary formula for $\kappa $.

  • The domain is $S^1$. These are compact, complete, proper immersions with self-intersections. Their curvature is positive after orienting the curve, so they are locally strictly convex; their images do not bound convex embedded domains.

  • The round circle is the only embedded closed shrinking curve. The noncircular curves have finite entropy strictly greater than two, as recorded in [CMComplexity2020], Section 6.3.

  • The conservation law $\kappa e^{-|X|^2/4}=C>0$ relates curvature to distance from the shrinking center. Compactness makes the shrinking flow a Type I flow becoming extinct at the origin.

The interactive figure starts with $(m,\ell )=(2,3)$. Enter other coprime indices in the stated range, or use the example buttons, to compute more numerical profiles. The viewer supports $m,\ell \leq 100$ and reports the numerical closing gap. This display limit is not a restriction of the classification theorem. The static drawing and printed figure show $(2,3)$. These computations illustrate the closing condition; their residuals are not certified error bounds.

The nonclosed irrational-turning branch is recorded separately in Example 2.11; see also [Halldorsson2012], Theorem 5.1.
