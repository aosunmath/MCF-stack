§ 4.7 Rotational graphical expanders over round conesA178E87D4BA0

(Entire radial cone smoothings) For $n\geq 2$ and $\kappa >0$, consider

\[ M_\kappa =\{ (x,\phi _\kappa (|x|)):x\in \mathbb R^ n\} \subset \mathbb R^{n+1}, \]

where $\phi _\kappa $ is the smooth radial solution constructed with

\[ \frac{\phi _\kappa ''}{1+(\phi _\kappa ')^2} +\frac{n-1}{r}\phi _\kappa ' +\frac{r\phi _\kappa '-\phi _\kappa }{2}=0, \qquad \phi _\kappa '(0)=0, \qquad \lim _{r\to \infty }\frac{\phi _\kappa (r)}r=\kappa . \]

The equation at $r=0$ is interpreted by smooth radial extension; it implies $\phi _\kappa ''(0)=\phi _\kappa (0)/(2n)$. This is an ODE construction, not a closed formula.

The hypersurface is smooth, complete, properly embedded, noncompact, rotationally symmetric, diffeomorphic to $\mathbb R^ n$, and $E$-minimizing against compact replacements. It has one conical end and nondecreasing height. The estimate

\[ 0\leq \phi _\kappa (r)-\kappa r \leq ((n+1)\kappa +2)\min \{ 1,r^{-1}\} ,\qquad r>0, \]

records the rate at which it approaches its cone. Its flow is $M_ t=\sqrt t\, M_\kappa $ for $t>0$, with initial cone $z=\kappa |x|$. Source: [Ding20], Section 4, equations (4.1), (4.7), and (4.8), printed pp. 371–373, supplied PDF pp. 13–15. The interactive figure shows a numerical rotational solution in $\mathbb R^3$ with $\phi (0)=1$.
