§ 2.25 Drugan–Kleene immersed shrinking familiesF2D20223E5E5

Paper credits and chronology

Paper credits are chronological and include every author of the cited papers. They identify sources, not exclusive discovery credit. Background-only citations do not determine the date of this example. A classical example may predate the references recorded here.

  1. Drugan–Kleene — 2013-06-10 (first arXiv submission).
F2D20223E5E5
C28E16016422

For every $n\geq 2$, Drugan–Kleene construct infinitely many complete rotational immersed self-shrinkers in $\mathbb R^{n+1}$ of each topology $S^ n$, $\mathbb R^ n$, $\mathbb R\times S^{n-1}$ and $S^1\times S^{n-1}$. This expands the single sphere construction in Example 2.24; it does not identify the torus examples with Angenent's embedded torus. The arclength meridian $(x,r)$ satisfies

\[ x'=\cos \alpha ,\quad r'=\sin \alpha ,\quad \alpha '=\frac{x}{2}\sin \alpha +\left(\frac{n-1}{r}-\frac r2\right)\cos \alpha . \]

A smooth axis endpoint must meet the axis orthogonally. Closed meridians generate tori; the other endpoint conditions distinguish spheres, planes and cylinders. The existence proof uses shooting and limiting geodesics; arbitrary initial data do not produce one of these complete examples. The flow is $M_ t=\sqrt{-t}\, \Sigma $, $-\infty <t<0$. Nontrivial rotational spheres, planes and cylinders in this construction are not embedded, by the rotational classification quoted in the source. No blanket embedding or uniqueness assertion is made for the torus branch.

Sources: [DruganKleene2017].
