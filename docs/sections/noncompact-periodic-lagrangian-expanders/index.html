§ 4.15 Noncompact periodic Lagrangian expanders49998F6A2866

Paper credits and chronology

Title credits include all authors of papers with a reviewed construction of this example, in chronological order. Later analysis, surveys, and citations remain listed here; they do not by themselves earn construction credit. Alternative and numerical constructions are qualified below. These credits are not an exhaustive discovery history. Background-only citations do not determine the date of this example. A classical example may predate the references recorded here.

  1. Joyce–Lee–Tsui — 2008-01-24 (first arXiv submission).
49998F6A2866
3823D6B50DAF

In Joyce–Lee–Tsui, Theorems E–F, choose $1\le m<n$, $\lambda _1=\cdots =\lambda _ m=1$, $\lambda _{m+1}=\cdots =\lambda _ n=-1$, and $C=1$. For the coupled ODE data of Theorems A–B the immersion is

\[ L=\{ (x_1w_1(s),\ldots ,x_ nw_ n(s)):\ \sum _ j\lambda _ jx_ j^2=1\} . \]

This entry records the nonconstant-amplitude periodic case. If all phase increments are rational multiples of $\pi $, the $w_ j$ have a common period and the source describes a closed noncompact immersed Lagrangian with parameter topology $S^1\times S^{m-1}\times \mathbb R^{n-m}$. For fixed sign of the soliton parameter such periodic initial data are dense in the allowed data. The $m=1$ parameter quadric has two components, and image identifications must be treated separately. The constant-amplitude Hamiltonian-stationary overlap is already included in the Lee–Wang quadric examples. For positive soliton parameter this is a self-expander; after normalization its flow is $\sqrt t\, L$, $t>0$. Periodic bounded nonvanishing amplitudes give completeness and properness of the resulting periodic immersion. The theorem does not assert that every such immersion is embedded. Nonperiodic quasiperiodic data instead give nonclosed images with larger-dimensional closures and are not assigned the same topology or properness. The figure is a phase-loop and indefinite-quadric schematic; it does not falsely replace the coupled ODE by independent sinusoidal phases.

Sources: [JoyceLeeTsui2010].
