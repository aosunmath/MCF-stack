§ 2.32 Compact periodic Lagrangian quadric shrinkers6AFBAD1A608F

6AFBAD1A608F These are the compact periodic solutions in the Joyce–Lee–Tsui construction.
98593B70A49E

(Periodic quadric shrinkers) Fix $n\geq 2$, $a_ j>0$, $\alpha =-1/2$, and put $Q(u)=\prod _{j=1}^ n(a_ j+u)$ and $\phi =\sum _ j\phi _ j$. Solve

\[ u'=2\sqrt{Q(u)}\cos (\phi -\theta ),\qquad \phi _ j'=-\frac{\sqrt{Q(u)}\sin (\phi -\theta )}{a_ j+u},\qquad \theta '=-\tfrac 12\sqrt{Q(u)}\sin (\phi -\theta ). \]

Require $a_ j+u>0$ and $A=\sqrt{Q(u)}e^{-u/4}\sin (\phi -\theta )>0$. Choose nonconstant periodic data for which all phase increments over a period of $u$ are rational multiples of $\pi $. A common period $T$ then closes all $w_ j(s)=\sqrt{a_ j+u(s)}e^{i\phi _ j(s)}$. The immersion is

\[ X(x,s)=(x_1w_1(s),\ldots ,x_ nw_ n(s)),\qquad (x,s)\in \mathbb S^{n-1}\times (\mathbb R/T\mathbb Z). \]

Theorems A, B, E(a)(ii), and F of [JoyceLeeTsui2010] establish these ODE solutions and a dense set of closing initial data. They satisfy $\vec H=-X^\perp /2$, and $\sqrt{-t}\, X$, $t<0$, is their mean curvature flow.

The parameter manifold is compact, hence the immersion is complete and proper. It has dimension and codimension $n$, no ends, and topology $\mathbb S^{n-1}\times \mathbb S^1$; for $n=2$ it is a torus. Coordinate reflections are symmetries. Embeddedness, uniqueness, and stability are not inferred from the immersion theorem. The drawing is a construction diagram, not a numerically closed solution. The constant-radius subcase, including familiar overlapping examples, is excluded here.
