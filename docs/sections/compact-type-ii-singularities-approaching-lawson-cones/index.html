§ 5.41 Compact type-II singularities approaching Lawson cones (Liu, 2024)0948A6AC6488

Paper credits and chronology

Title credits include all authors of papers with a reviewed construction of this example, in chronological order. Later analysis, surveys, and citations remain listed here; they do not by themselves earn construction credit. Alternative and numerical constructions are qualified below. These credits are not an exhaustive discovery history. Background-only citations do not determine the date of this example. A classical example may predate the references recorded here.

  1. Liu — 2024-03-25 (first arXiv submission). Construction: Preprint construction of compact bounded-mean-curvature singularities; no independent proof verification. Passage: Theorem 1.4; Section 4 initial capped profile and Theorem 4.5; Corollary 8.5.
0948A6AC6488
81D16D031DF7

(Liu's compact flows with bounded mean curvature) Liu's 2024 preprint constructs smooth compact embedded hypersurface flows $M_ t^{N-1}\subset \mathbb R^ N$, $t_0\leq t<0$, for every $N\geq 8$, with a type-II singularity and uniformly bounded scalar mean curvature. Choose integers $p,q\geq 2$ with $p+q=N$; when $N=8$ require $p,q\geq 3$. The symmetry is $O(p)\times O(q)$, and the local cone is

\[ C_{p,q}=\{ (x,y)\in \mathbb R^ p\times \mathbb R^ q: |y|=\mu |x|\} ,\qquad \mu =\sqrt{\frac{q-1}{p-1}}. \]

Thus $p=q$ recovers the equal-factor Simons cone. These restrictions on the blocks are part of the construction; a flat-product extension of a noncompact example would not produce a compact example.

Near the singular region the positive profile $Q$ satisfies

\[ Q_ t=\frac{Q_{rr}}{1+Q_ r^2}+\frac{p-1}{r}Q_ r-\frac{q-1}{Q}, \qquad Q_ r(0,t)=0. \]

The outer profile is capped smoothly. In the source's construction one sphere factor collapses at each endpoint of a simple orbit-space interval. The resulting join $S^{p-1}*S^{q-1}$ gives topology $S^{N-1}$. This is a deduction from Section 4's parametrization. Every regular slice is complete and proper by compactness, with no ends; the mixed curvature near the minimal model precludes global convexity there.

With

\[ \alpha _ N=\frac{3-N+\sqrt{N^2-10N+17}}2,\qquad \beta _\ell =\frac{\ell }{1-\alpha _ N}, \]

the selected flows converge to $C_{p,q}$ under the scale $(-t)^{-1/2}$ away from its vertex, and locally smoothly to its smooth minimal desingularization under $(-t)^{-\beta _\ell }$. The bounded-mean-curvature ranges are

\[ N=8:\ \ell \geq 4;\qquad N\geq 9:\ \ell \geq 3, \]

with integer $\ell $ and no parity requirement in this preprint. The second fundamental form becomes unbounded although $\sup _{t_0\leq t<0}\sup _{M_ t}|H|<\infty $. This is a finite-time construction, not an ancient flow or a uniqueness theorem.

Source: [Liu2024], Theorem 1.4, Section 2, the admissibility conditions in Section 3, the capped initial profile in Section 4, Theorem 4.5, Proposition 8.1 and Corollary 8.5 (arXiv v1 PDF pp. 3–4, 8–10, 12–17, 48–54). The result is recorded as a preprint theorem, without independent proof verification. Each compact smooth slice has finite Gaussian area and entropy, but no numerical value or uniform-in-time entropy bound is assigned. The numerical profile shows only the local minimal model and its asymptotic scales; it does not compute the compact cap or the degree-selected PDE solution. Compare 5.37 and 5.36.
