§ 5.1 The Angenent oval as an explicit ancient flow34E98635F82B

34E98635F82B
B008243C5D96

(The ancient paperclip curve) With the speed-one curve-shortening convention, the exact implicit equation

\[ \Gamma _ t=\{ (x,y)\in \mathbb R^2:|x|<\pi /2,\ e^ t\cosh y=\cos x\} , \qquad -\infty <t<0, \]

defines the Angenent oval. Equivalently, its two graph branches are

\[ y=\pm \operatorname {arcosh}(e^{-t}\cos x), \qquad |x|\leq \arccos (e^ t). \]

Their endpoints join smoothly as a curve, even though those graph charts cease to be regular there. This is an exact implicit formula, rather than a single global graph parametrization. It is recorded in [BV11], Section 3.1, equation (14), p. 7.

Every time slice is a smooth compact embedded strictly convex curve, hence complete and proper, with topology $S^1$ and reflection symmetries across both coordinate axes. It shrinks to the origin at time zero but changes shape and is not a soliton. Its enclosed area is $-2\pi t$, and

\[ \kappa _{\max }=(1-e^{2t})^{-1/2},\qquad \kappa _{\min }=e^ t(1-e^{2t})^{-1/2}. \]

The rescaling $(-t)^{-1/2}\Gamma _ t$ converges smoothly to the radius-$\sqrt2$ circle at extinction. As $t\to -\infty $, translating its upper and lower branches to keep their tips fixed gives the two oppositely directed grim-reaper profiles, locally in $|x|<\pi /2$. These limits can be read from the implicit equation: near extinction its quadratic expansion gives $x^2+y^2\sim -2t$, and subtracting the upper tip height gives the limiting graph $\log \cos x$ as $t\to -\infty $. The time slider plots the exact implicit curve for $-5\leq t\leq -0.1$ on a fixed spatial scale.
