§ 4.12 Lee–Wang embedded quadric expanders3A7601A9FD31

3A7601A9FD31
A1A5802C8577

(The expanding mixed-sign quadric family) For $n\geq 3$, $n/2<k<n$, and $S=2k-n$, set

\[ Q_-=\left\{ x\in \mathbb R^ n:\sum _{j\leq k}x_ j^2-\sum _{j>k}x_ j^2=-2S\right\} ,\qquad X(x,s)=(e^{is}x_1,\ldots ,e^{is}x_ k,e^{-is}x_{k+1},\ldots ,e^{-is}x_ n). \]

On $(Q_-\times \mathbb R)/((x,s)\sim (-x,s+\pi ))$ this is a complete proper embedded Lagrangian in $\mathbb C^ n$, of dimension and codimension $n$. It satisfies $\vec H=X^\perp /2$, and $L_ t=\sqrt t\, X(Q_-\times \mathbb R)$ for $t>0$. This is an exact parametrization subject to one quadric constraint, not an ODE approximation. See [LeeWang2010], Propositions 2.1–2.2, with weights $+1$ and $-1$.

The fiber is $\mathbb R^ k\times S^{n-k-1}$. The mapping-torus quotient is connected and has one end. If $n-k=1$, its two fiber components are exchanged by the gluing, and the total manifold is diffeomorphic to $\mathbb R^ k\times S^1$, hence orientable. For $n-k\geq 2$ the fiber is connected, the antipodal monodromy must be retained, and the total manifold is orientable exactly when $n$ is even. In particular, the $n=3,k=2$ case is orientable: one must pass around the base twice to return to the same component. These topology statements are obtained from the quotient directly; the product and parity shorthand in the source's Proposition 2.2 requires this disconnected-fiber qualification.

The family is noncompact and Hamiltonian stationary, with $SO(k)\times SO(n-k)$ and circle symmetries, and is asymptotic to the same cone as Example 2.28. Its planar-coordinate quadric description gives polynomial volume growth and finite centered Gaussian area; this entry records no entropy value or stability result. The illustrated $s=0$ slice has two sheets, which belong to a single connected expander after circle gluing. No surface genus is assigned to these $n\geq 3$ examples. The source's weak continuation at $t=0$ is a separate varifold statement; the smooth time interval here is $t>0$.

Ambient spaces: Higher dimensions (N ≥ 5)
Ambient space, topology, embeddedness and entropy

Full Euclidean ambient dimension is 2n or 2m, at least six. Auxiliary slices and curves do not determine the genus of this higher-dimensional example.

Embedded unit-weight expanders in Cⁿ, n ≥ 3, n/2 < k < n · Euclidean dimension ≥ 5 · Embedded

Topology
Mapping torus of Rᵏ × Sⁿ⁻ᵏ⁻¹ under antipodal gluing; Rᵏ × S¹ when n − k = 1.

Embedded: Embedded for this explicitly restricted case; no claim about other weights, groups, or singular limits.
Compactness
Noncompact
Surface genus
Intrinsic dimension at least three; surface genus is inapplicable.
Topological ends
One end; two fiber components in the n − k = 1 case are joined by the gluing.
Entropy
Not recorded: No value or bound recorded from the checked source; entropy is not inferred from a single centered Gaussian integral.
Gaussian area
Finite; value not recorded: Finite by the explicit quadric geometry and polynomial volume growth; no value recorded.
