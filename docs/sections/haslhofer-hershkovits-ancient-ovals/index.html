§ 5.8 Haslhofer–Hershkovits ancient ovals12F33FD70B5C

12F33FD70B5C
56D296501918

For $n\geq 2$ and every $1\leq j\leq n-1$, Haslhofer–Hershkovits construct an ancient hypersurface flow $M_ t\subset \mathbb R^{n+1}$, $-\infty <t<0$, with compact strictly convex embedded slices. Each slice is diffeomorphic to $S^ n$, hence complete and proper with no ends. The construction has $O(j+1)\times O(n-j)$ symmetry and is uniformly Andrews noncollapsed. It is not self-similar and becomes round at extinction.

Under backward parabolic blow-down,

\[ \lambda ^{-1}M_{\lambda ^2t}\longrightarrow S^ j(\sqrt{2j|t|})\times \mathbb R^{n-j}\quad (\lambda \to \infty ). \]

Tip rescalings approach $\mathrm{Bowl}^{j+1}\times \mathbb R^{n-j-1}$, with the bowl normalized to unit tip curvature. Rescaled diameter is unbounded backward in time and curvature decay is type II. No closed formula or uniqueness follows from this construction. Gaussian area and entropy are finite for each compact slice; no fixed slice value is evaluated here.

Source: [HH13], Theorem 1.1 and Section 3. Huisken–Sinestrari's Theorem 3.2 in [HS14] shows why the hypothesis $h_{ij}\geq \varepsilon H g_{ij}$ with one $\varepsilon >0$ for all ancient times cannot hold for these nonspherical ovals: a closed convex ancient flow with that uniform pinching is a shrinking sphere. The displayed elongated shape is heuristic, not an evolving ellipsoid solution.
