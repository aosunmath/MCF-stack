§ 5.20 Lagrangian sphere chains in Gibbons–Hawking spaces2AB1CBD37191

2AB1CBD37191 These are surface flows of codimension two in a hyperkahler four-manifold.
DE8829990703

(Infinite-time curvature blow-up with vanishing mean curvature) Use the Gibbons–Hawking setting of [LeeTsai2026], Section 2: the circle quotient contains the collinear centers $(a_ j,0,0)$ with $-1=a_1<\cdots <a_ N=1$, $N\geq 3$, and

\[ \varphi =\varphi _{\rm reg}+\frac12\sum _{j=1}^ N ((x-a_ j)^2+y^2+z^2)^{-1/2},\qquad g=\varphi ^{-1}\eta ^2+\varphi (dx^2+dy^2+dz^2), \quad d\eta =*d\varphi . \]

The regular part is smooth, nonnegative, and harmonic on the source's quotient neighborhood. Let the initial Lagrangian sphere be the smooth circle lift of a positive concave graph $(x,u_0(x),0)$ joining the outer centers, with $u_0(\pm 1)=0$. For fixed $\Lambda >0$, require the small-slope and curvature conditions of Theorem 6.2:

\[ |u_0'|\leq c^{-1},\qquad -u_0''\leq \Lambda u_0\varphi (x,u_0,0), \]

where $c$ is sufficiently large as supplied by that theorem. The reduced equation is

\[ u_ t=\frac{u_{xx}}{\varphi (x,u,0)(1+u_ x^2)},\qquad u(\pm 1,t)=0. \]

The lifted flow exists smoothly for $t\geq 0$, remains a compact embedded sphere, and converges to the $A_{N-1}$ chain of $N-1$ special Lagrangian spheres. Theorem 6.2 proves

\[ \sup |\vec H|\longrightarrow 0,\qquad 0<\liminf _{t\to \infty }\frac{\log \max |A|}{\sqrt t} \leq \limsup _{t\to \infty }\frac{\log \max |A|}{\sqrt t}<\infty . \]

Finite-time slices have genus zero, no ends, and are complete and proper. Junctions occur only in the infinite-time limit. The quotient drawing is schematic, not a numerical solution of this weighted equation. It is not ordinary planar curve shortening, and Euclidean entropy is inapplicable. The coauthor is Ping-Hung Lee, distinct from Tang-Kai Lee.
