§ 5.6 Eternal shrinking helices281263153F94

281263153F94
1EF23C4EFFC0

(Helical curve-shortening flows) Fix $m\geq 1$ and integers $1\leq k_1<\cdots <k_ m$. Let $r(t)>0$, $t\in \mathbb R$, solve the exact implicit equation

\[ \log r(t)+\frac12\sum _{j=1}^ m r(t)^{2k_ j^2}=\frac m2-t. \]

The choice of constant makes $r(0)=1$. Then

\[ \Gamma _ t(s)= \bigl (r^{k_1^2}\cos (k_1s),r^{k_1^2}\sin (k_1s),\ldots , r^{k_ m^2}\cos (k_ ms),r^{k_ m^2}\sin (k_ ms),s\bigr ), \qquad s\in \mathbb R, \]

is an eternal curve-shortening flow in $\mathbb R^{2m+1}$. In the simplest case $m=1$, $k_1=1$, it is the three-dimensional helix

\[ \Gamma _ t(s)=(r(t)\cos s,r(t)\sin s,s),\qquad \log r(t)+r(t)^2/2=1/2-t. \]

These are exact parametrizations with an implicitly determined time scale, not numerical ODE profiles. The ordinary differential equation is

\[ r'=-\frac{r}{1+\sum _ j k_ j^2r^{2k_ j^2}}. \]

The last coordinate makes every time slice a proper embedding of $\mathbb R$, and the induced metric is $(1+\sum _ jk_ j^2r^{2k_ j^2})ds^2$, so it is complete. It is noncompact with two ends and codimension $2m$, and it spans the stated ambient space. Simultaneous rotations together with translation along the last coordinate give its screw symmetry. Its radius decreases to zero as $t\to +\infty $, giving local smooth convergence to the axis, while $r(t)\to \infty $ as $t\to -\infty $. At any fixed finite time the ends continue winding; they are not asymptotic to the axis as $|s|\to \infty $.

Proposition 2.7 of [SS19] proves

\[ \sup _{t\in \mathbb R}\lambda (\Gamma _ t)=+\infty . \]

This concerns the entire time family and does not say that every individual slice has infinite entropy. In fact, a direct calculation gives the exact fixed-time entropy below. Set $B=\sum _ jr^{2k_ j^2}$ and $D=\sum _ jk_ j^2r^{2k_ j^2}$. Direct Gaussian integration, using $|\Gamma _ t(s)|^2=B+s^2$, gives the exact centered value

\[ F_{0,1}(\Gamma _ t)=\sqrt{1+D}\, e^{-B/4}; \qquad F_{0,1}(\Gamma _ t)=\sqrt{1+r^2}\, e^{-r^2/4} \quad \hbox{when }m=k_1=1. \]

For completeness, the same calculation at arbitrary Gaussian scale gives

\[ \lambda (\Gamma _ t)=\sqrt{1+D(t)}. \]

Indeed, for a center $(a,z_0)\in \mathbb R^{2m}\times \mathbb R$, dropping the nonnegative squared distance in the first $2m$ coordinates bounds $F_{(a,z_0),\tau }$ by $(4\pi \tau )^{-1/2}\sqrt{1+D}\int _{\mathbb R}e^{-(s-z_0)^2/(4\tau )}\, ds=\sqrt{1+D}$. At the origin, $F_{0,\tau }=\sqrt{1+D}e^{-B/(4\tau )}$ tends to this upper bound as $\tau \to \infty $. Thus every finite-time helix has finite entropy, while the time supremum is infinite. This is a direct integration, additional to the cited time-supremum result.

Source: [SS19], equation (2.6), Propositions 2.6–2.7, and Theorem A.2, PDF pp. 6–7 and 9; see also [AAAW13], Section 3, PDF pp. 8–10. The family is eternal even though a display uses only finitely many turns.
