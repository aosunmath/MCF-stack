§ 3.35 Parabolic Killing grim reapers in hyperbolic space2C2BD4493223

Paper credits and chronology

Title credits include all authors of papers with a reviewed construction of this example, in chronological order. Later analysis, surveys, and citations remain listed here; they do not by themselves earn construction credit. Alternative and numerical constructions are qualified below. These credits are not an exhaustive discovery history. Background-only citations do not determine the date of this example. A classical example may predate the references recorded here.

  1. Bueno–López — 2024-02-08 (first arXiv submission).
2C2BD4493223
69BB4AD6B5E2

Bueno–López construct a family of grim-reaper analogues in $\mathbb H^3$ driven by the parabolic Killing field $\xi =a\partial _ x+b\partial _ y$ in the upper-half-space model. The metric is $z^{-2}(dx^2+dy^2+dz^2)$ and the isometries are $(x,y,z)\mapsto (x+a\tau ,y+b\tau ,z)$. The construction classifies the relevant invariant surfaces via a planar ODE. It differs from the hyperbolic-axis translation family, whose half-space coordinates dilate. With the average-curvature convention in this paper, the corresponding trace-MCF time is $\tau =2t$. The ambient isometry group exists for all real time. The nontrivial profiles and their domains are those of Section 3; no uniform embedding or completeness assertion is inferred for arbitrary initial data of the ODE. The rotational no-go results of Section 2 eliminate other proposed invariant cases rather than constructing additional solitons. The figure draws the asymptotic shape and horizontal Killing direction in a fixed half-space chart; it is schematic and is not a Euclidean grim curve asserted to solve the hyperbolic equation.

Sources: [BuenoLopez2024a].
