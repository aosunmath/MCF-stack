§ 3.9 Nguyen translating tridents31505E08B028

31505E08B028
27C982677562

Nguyen's tridents are complete periodic embedded translators in $\mathbb R^3$ constructed by desingularizing the intersection of a grim-reaper cylinder and a plane. There is no explicit global parametrization in the supplied survey.

  • They are noncompact and have infinite genus. The construction uses repeated small connecting regions; a smooth union of the input grim cylinder and plane is not itself the translator.

  • The supplied notes compare their asymptotics with the pitchfork: in the upward convention there is a single asymptotic plane in the negative-height direction and three parallel asymptotic planes in the positive-height direction.

  • They generate eternal flows by translation. Periodicity and infinite genus distinguish them from the simply connected pitchfork.

See [HIMWNotes19], Section 4, PDF page 11, and Section 5, PDF page 12, referring to [Nguyen09]. The construction is externally cited; the survey does not specify a full parameter range or a uniqueness, entropy, or stability result. A drawing of the intersecting plane and grim cylinder illustrates the gluing configuration only.
