§ 5.37 Low-mode type-II singularities approaching Simons cones (Velázquez, 1994)FA0AF7B1B843

Paper credits and chronology

Title credits include all authors of papers with a reviewed construction of this example, in chronological order. Later analysis, surveys, and citations remain listed here; they do not by themselves earn construction credit. Alternative and numerical constructions are qualified below. These credits are not an exhaustive discovery history. Background-only citations do not determine the date of this example. A classical example may predate the references recorded here.

  1. Velázquez — 1994 (publication year). Construction: Constructs the degree-selected type-II family; subsequent mean-curvature conclusions are credited separately in the text. Passage: Proposition 2.2, Theorems 2.1–2.2; printed pp. 607–611.
  2. Guo–Šešum — 2017-01-07 (first arXiv submission). Analysis: Refines convergence and proves slower mean-curvature blow-up for n ≥ 5 and k = 2; analyzes Velázquez’s family. Passage: Theorem 1.2, Theorem 4.8 and Propositions 5.1–5.4.
  3. Stolarski — 2020-03-13 (first arXiv submission). Analysis: Restates the mode-indexed construction and proves smooth inner convergence. Passage: Theorems 3.1 and 3.3.

(Velázquez flows and the Guo–Šešum blow-up analysis) Write $\mathbb R^{2n}=\mathbb R^ n\times \mathbb R^ n$, with $n\geq 4$, and let

\[ C_ n=\{ (x,y):|x|=|y|\} ,\qquad M_ t=\{ (r\omega ,Q(r,t)\theta ):r\geq 0,\ \omega ,\theta \in S^{n-1}\} . \]

The cone is singular at its vertex. The constructed $M_ t$, on a finite interval $t_0\leq t<0$, are smooth complete properly embedded noncompact hypersurfaces of dimension $2n-1$ and codimension one. Their $O(n)\times O(n)$ symmetry reduces MCF to

\[ Q_ t=\frac{Q_{rr}}{1+Q_ r^2}+\frac{n-1}{r}Q_ r-\frac{n-1}{Q}, \qquad Q(0,t)>0,\quad Q_ r(0,t)=0. \]

At the axis the first two terms have the combined limit $nQ_{rr}(0,t)$. The positive radial graph gives topology $\mathbb R^ n\times S^{n-1}$ and one end. These are topological consequences of the parametrization, not surface-genus assertions. They are not ancient solutions or solitons, and no closed formula for $Q(r,t)$ is known from these sources.

Set

\[ \alpha _ n=\frac{-(2n-3)+\sqrt{4n^2-20n+17}}2, \qquad \lambda _ k=k+\frac{\alpha _ n-1}{2}, \qquad \beta _ k=\frac{k}{1-\alpha _ n},\quad \sigma _ k=\beta _ k-\frac12. \]

Velázquez constructs a discrete family indexed by integers $k\geq 2$. This page records its low mode $k=2$; the higher modes with bounded mean curvature are recorded in 5.36. At the parabolic scale $(-t)^{-1/2}M_ t$, the limit is $C_ n$ on compact annuli away from the vertex. At the finer scale $(-t)^{-\beta _ k}M_ t$, the limit is a smooth minimal hypersurface asymptotic to $C_ n$. Its profile, normalized here by $P(0)=1$, solves

\[ \frac{P''}{1+(P')^2}+(n-1)\left(\frac{P'}r-\frac1P\right)=0, \qquad P'(0)=0,\qquad P(r)=r+c r^{\alpha _ n}+o(r^{\alpha _ n}),\quad c>0. \]

Other normalizations are $P_ b(r)=bP(r/b)$. The ODE, not a closed formula, specifies this minimal model. Its convex profile does not make the full hypersurface convex: the two spherical blocks contribute principal curvatures of opposite signs.

Guo–Šešum prove for $n\geq 5$, $k=2$, and their choice of exterior initial data that the origin is the only singular point at time zero, that the finer rescalings converge locally smoothly, and that

\[ 0<\limsup _{t\uparrow 0}(-t)^{\beta _2}\sup _{M_ t}|A|<\infty . \]

The mean curvature also blows up, but more slowly. More precisely, for some $C(n)>0$ and $0<\varrho (n)<1$,

\[ \limsup _{t\uparrow 0}(-t)^{1/2-\sigma _2} \sup _{M_ t\cap B(0,C(n)(-t)^{\beta _2})}|H|>0, \qquad \limsup _{t\uparrow 0}(-t)^{1/2+(1-2\varrho )\sigma _2}\sup _{M_ t}|H|<\infty . \]

These are different lower and upper estimates, not an exact asymptotic equivalent for $|H|$. The stated Guo–Šešum theorem excludes $n=4$; existence of the $k=2$ flow in $\mathbb R^8$ must not be read as this same mean-curvature conclusion.

Sources: [Velazquez1994], Proposition 2.1, Proposition 2.2 and Theorems 2.1–2.2, printed pp. 605–611; [GuoSesum2018], Theorem 1.2, Section 2, equation (3.5), Theorem 4.8 and Propositions 5.1–5.4; [Stolarski2023], Theorems 3.1 and 3.3. Existence is selected by a degree argument; no general uniqueness or dynamical stability is asserted. No entropy or centered Gaussian-area value of the evolving slices is evaluated here. The figure integrates the minimal ODE and applies the source's asymptotic scales. A time-dependent dilation of this minimal leaf alone is not an MCF solution.
