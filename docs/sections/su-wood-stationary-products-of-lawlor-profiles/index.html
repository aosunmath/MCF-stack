§ 5.15 Su–Wood stationary products of Lawlor profilesC99B5440B24D

C99B5440B24D
3CC118326D79

(Cohomogeneity-two special Lagrangians) Let $m\geq 3$, $d=m-1$, $I=(-\pi /(2d),\pi /(2d))$, $\mu _1,\mu _2>0$, and $\varphi \in \mathbb R$. Define

\[ r(s)=(\cos (ds))^{-1/d},\quad \ell (s)=r(s)e^{is},\quad B(s)=\int _0^ s r(v)^2\, dv. \]

For $u\in S^{m-2}\subset \mathbb R^{m-1}$ and $(x,y)\in I^2$, put

\[ X(u,x,y)=\left(e^{i\varphi }\mu _1\mu _2\ell (x)\ell (y)u, \frac{\mu _2^2r(y)^2-\mu _1^2r(x)^2}{2} +i\bigl (\mu _2^2B(y)-\mu _1^2B(x)\bigr )\right)\in \mathbb C^ m. \]

This is an exact integral parametrization of an embedded, exact special Lagrangian of dimension and codimension $m$. Thus $\vec H=0$ and $L_ t=X(S^{m-2}\times I^2)$ for every $t\in \mathbb R$. It is the $G=SO(m-1)$ case of [SuWood2026], equation (31), Theorem 5.6, Proposition 5.9 and Remark 5.10. It has cohomogeneity two under this group; for $m=3$ the topology is $S^1\times \mathbb R^2$, not a surface annulus.

The topology in general is $S^{m-2}\times \mathbb R^2$, with one end. The parametrization is proper: approaching either endpoint of $I$ makes $|\ell (x)\ell (y)|$ diverge, and the remaining factor $u$ is compact. The induced metric is therefore complete. The calibrated image is stable under compactly supported variations. No entropy value or global asymptotic classification is recorded here. The picture is the exact one-dimensional Lawlor profile used twice by the construction, not a projection of the full $m$-manifold.

The same paper constructs translators from a line and an Anciaux shrinker profile, and from an expander/shrinker pair. The line/expander case recovers the symmetric Joyce–Lee–Tsui translator of Example 3.17; it is not a second catalogue entry. For other symmetry groups some constructions have a one-dimensional singular set, so their smooth embeddedness must not be inferred from this $SO(m-1)$ case.
