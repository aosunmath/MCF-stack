Browse by properties

Find special flows by their ambient space, topology, embeddedness, and Gaussian area or entropy. Filters are combined within the same dimensional case of a family.

Conventions and how to read the data

Topology refers to the parameter manifold for an immersion and to a regular time slice for an evolving flow. Surface genus applies only in dimension two. Ends are topological ends; several asymptotic sheets in one height direction need not be different ends. For disconnected examples the text states whether a count is per component. Ambient space means the space stated in the construction, rather than every possible Euclidean inclusion.

Embeddedness describes the stated immersion or regular time slices in the full ambient space, not a projection or a singular or infinite-time limit. “Immersed” alone does not mean “not embedded.” Multiple covers are not embeddings. “Depends on the construction” identifies families whose status depends on their factors or input immersion; these do not match either definite filter. “Not recorded” means no conclusion is recorded here. Networks are called embedded when their branches meet only at the prescribed junctions; they are not smooth curves at those junctions.

For an n-dimensional Euclidean submanifold, including immersion multiplicity,

\[F_{x_0,\tau}(M)=(4\pi\tau)^{-n/2}\int_M e^{-|X-x_0|^2/(4\tau)}\,d\mu,\qquad \lambda(M)=\sup_{x_0,\,\tau>0}F_{x_0,\tau}(M).\]

Centered Gaussian area means F₀,₁ of the stated slice. It generally differs from entropy and depends on center and scale. For the normalized complete shrinkers with polynomial area growth here, F₀,₁ equals entropy. A bound on the supremum over a flow's times is labeled separately from the entropy of a single slice. Euclidean entropy is not assigned to an intrinsic flow in a curved ambient manifold.

“Not recorded” means this catalogue has no value or theorem recorded, not that none is known. The proved-entropy-bound filter uses recorded scalar values and upper bounds; a parameter-dependent formula without a recorded uniform bound will not match. Numerical estimates are excluded. A family may appear in several groups. References and hypotheses are in the linked entries.
